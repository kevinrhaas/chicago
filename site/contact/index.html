Bring us a place.

4D Chicago grows one source at a time. If you hold a piece of the city's record, or want to put the city to work, write to us. We read every message.

  • SourcesA photograph, a letter, a deed or a family story about a building in 1812, 1835 or 1904
  • ArchivesLibraries and historical societies with collections we should be citing
  • ClassroomsSchools, museums and exhibits that want to walk the city with their visitors
  • CorrectionsIf the record says otherwise, so will the model
