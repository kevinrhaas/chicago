# Evidence and geometry contract

Keep five dates distinct: publication, depicted/surveyed condition, construction, alteration and loss. A scene year is a filter over phases, never a command to relabel later material as contemporary. 1904 has no chosen month/day in this library; the Robbins completion and Glessner workbench within the year remain unresolved.

## Layers and confidence

1. Named-building history: source-backed associations and event text, preserving disputed dates.
2. Map frontage: 1911 observations, not independently resolved cadastral entities. Keep raw addresses and story/basement notation.
3. Directory candidates: 1904-edition Blue Book listings, OCR-derived and explicitly selective. It reflects an autumn canvass/copyright 1903. Page-level visual verification is still required.
4. Measured drawings and surveys: record survey date, sheet/page and alteration state before geometry extraction.
5. Census, permits and civic rules: catalog discovery is not document reading. No permit number, census person or block-specific paving material is invented here.

Exact four-digit dates can drive the viewer; ranges, decades and conflicts remain text with incomplete bounds. No deletion is inferred from a missing directory entry. Predecessors and moved houses are excluded from the 1904 site when the evidence says so. Rees remains at 2110 in 1904, not its modern 2017 address; Clarke's current site is not a 1904 building location.

## Measurement procedure

Use original image pixels and the printed graphical scale, rectify skew with at least four well-distributed controls, and check independent distances. Preserve the transformation, residuals, units and uncertainty. The 66-foot map annotations describe the street corridor, not a measured curb-to-curb carriageway. Story counts are not building heights. Blue may mean stone construction or only a narrow stone front; check the key and the drawn extent. Do not turn map wall-thickness figures into facade widths.

A later HABS measured plan is a measured later condition. Align it to dated period photographs, plans, permits and the Sanborn footprint before backcasting. Present-day GIS footprints cannot replace lost 1904 parcel boundaries. No coordinates, roof pitches or footprint polygons have been fabricated. Geographic data remain unrectified.

## Rights and acquisition

Original maps dated 1886/1911 are retained unchanged with provenance. Custodian photographs, modern nominations and museum reproductions retain item-level rights caveats. Rights-uncertain archival originals are held in the companion research archive, not turned into textures, model derivatives or public gallery thumbnails. Only explicitly identified government survey material may be mirrored as a local report. Every acquired file has bytes/hash; failed HTTP retrievals stay marked failed. Discovery URLs with no bytes are references, not stored documents.
