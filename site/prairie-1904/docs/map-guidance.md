# Supplied map guidance — 23 September 2026

Source: William Tyre's note and five map files supplied by Kevin Haas for this project. This is a factual summary; the correspondence itself is not republished.

The supplied 1911 Sanborn set covers 16th–18th (sheet 20), 18th–20th (28), and 20th–22nd (35), with the key for symbols and materials. Tyre identifies two physical changes from the 1904 target: the paired houses at 1609–1611 were replaced by a commercial building, and the house at 1620 was demolished with its lot left vacant. The supplied 1886 Robinson detail supplies their earlier outlines, at lower detail.

Retain this as attributed expert guidance. It supports a working backcast of the footprint layer, not a claim that every occupant, interior, garage conversion, utility or use remained unchanged. The 1911 medical college, dressmaking and institute labels remain 1911 observations until dated separately. The 1886 sheet is used only for the specified lost buildings, not as the complete 1904 scene.

The map-reader identifies 1609/1611 as the paired yellow rectangles on lot 6; the adjacent pink pair on lot 7 must not be substituted. At 1620, the earlier pink irregular building spans lots 13–14. These remain tentative visual readings pending rectification and a second check.
