# Site webfonts

Copies of the 4D app's Control Room interface faces (chicago/4d/renderers/web/fonts/), so the
public site matches the app without a third-party font request: Barlow, Barlow Semi Condensed,
IBM Plex Mono and Instrument Serif, latin subsets of the Google Fonts families. Every family is
licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).
