# Source acquisition register

## Acquired for this package

- **A. T. Andreas, *History of Chicago*, volume III (1886)** — public-domain Internet Archive scan and OCR. The working research cache contains full OCR; the package retains selected printed-page derivatives for the first-year rebuilding and 1877–1879 permit tables.
- **City of Chicago Individual Landmarks** — current municipal spatial dataset used for names, construction ranges, architects, addresses, and candidate geometry. It is a landmark subset, not a citywide historical inventory.
- **Annual maps, 1872–1879** — nine public-domain city maps/views sourced through Wikimedia Commons and credited to their holding collections. The package includes practical derivatives and direct archival/source URLs.
- **Supplied repository chronology and guide** — treated as lead lists only. Annual researchers verified, corrected, or excluded entries rather than inheriting dates uncritically.

## Reproducibility notes

Map checksums are generated at validation time in `maps/image_checksums.csv`. Source URLs, access dates, source types, reliability tiers, locators, rights notes, and research notes are normalized in `data/sources.csv`. The local full-text research cache is intentionally not required for database import.

Archival masters should be reacquired from the source URL before georeferencing or publication-quality reproduction. Reduced local images are navigation/research derivatives.
