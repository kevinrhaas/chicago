# Aggregate rebuilding and permit statistics audit

Status: **passed with one preserved source discrepancy**.

## Checks against Andreas volume III printed scans

- 70 unique estimate records parse to the exact 15-column schema and resolve to `ANDREAS_V3_REBUILD`.
- December 1, 1871–October 1, 1872 material classes reconcile to 1,250: 65 frame + 965 brick + 20 iron + 200 stone.
- The same period's story classes reconcile to 1,250: 284 one-story + 378 two-story + 226 three-story + 263 four-story + 88 five-story + 10 six-story + 1 seven-story.
- South, North, and West division first-year costs reconcile to the printed whole-burned-district total: $38,134,700 + $6,425,000 + $998,500 = $45,558,200 nominal.
- 1877 height classes and brick/stone fronts each reconcile to 1,398 buildings.
- 1878 height classes and brick/stone fronts each reconcile to 1,019 buildings.
- 1879 brick/stone fronts reconcile to 1,093 buildings.

## Preserved 1879 discrepancy

The printed 1879 height-class row reads 307 one-story, 455 two-story, 229 three-story, 16 four-story, and 3 five-story buildings: **1,010**, or 83 below the printed annual total of 1,093. The scan visually supports 307, not a silently “corrected” 390. All five height rows are therefore flagged for review and the missing 83 are not imputed.

## Interpretation controls

- These are permit/building-flow measurements, not a census of standing stock.
- First-year figures explicitly omit temporary unpermitted shanties and innumerable North Side cottages described by Andreas.
- Use classifications on printed page 67 are partial and do not partition every annual building.
- Historical dollar values remain nominal.
- “Prominent-building frontage” is not interchangeable with all-building frontage.

No anonymous building entities were generated from any aggregate value.
