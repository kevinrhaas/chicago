What still needs correction or reconciliation

  • Lifted copper at the northeast courtyard corner: confirmed local attachment/fold defect, assigned to T-2220. Keep the roof envelope while flattening the cladding onto its host.
  • Main roof and stair-turret rises: the 1963 section gives smaller rises than the current model. The later photogrammetric elevation supplies a different main-roof control. Retain the elevation-based ridge pending a common-datum reconciliation.
  • Photo controls: large tower and chimney residuals remain. The fixed camera assumptions, finial-tip definitions and roof associations need cross-view review before converting them into dimensional corrections.
  • Dining-bay description: the existing outline projects 9.90 ft, while its older prose says 10.3 ft. The audit uses the coordinates; the discrepancy does not establish a geometry error.

Unbuilt central gables, watercolor conservatory and fountain remain excluded. No pre-1904 courtyard photograph or unobscured whole-west view has been recovered. Later ivy, utilities and replacement fittings do not become 1904 facts.
