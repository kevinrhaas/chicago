4D Chicago

What we keep

You sign in once to walk the town. Here is everything 4D Chicago saves about you, why, and how to delete it.

What is saved to your account

  • Your visit record: your email, your name if your Google or Microsoft account gives one, how you signed in, the years you have entered, and when you first and last came in.
  • Each entry into a year: which year, when, and whether you were on a computer, tablet or phone.
  • Your reading age, so another device opens at the same level.
  • Your own person (how you look in the town), so it follows you.
  • Bug reports you send, with what you wrote and, if you chose, a picture of the screen.
  • Your field notebook, the people you have met, the places you have found and your settings, so they follow you to another device. Your screen's own graphics settings stay on that device. Signing in with your email alone keeps them in this browser only.
  • Your class, if you join one with your teacher's code: the name you give your teacher, when you joined and were last in, and the notebook entries you choose to hand in. Only your teacher sees them, and you can take them back. A teacher's class keeps its name, year group and code until the teacher closes it, which deletes it.
  • Cloud play rights, if the owner gave them to your sign-in address: the address, the rights, his note, and when.
  • The cloud play waitlist, if you join it: your address and name, the year you had chosen, what the system check said about your computer, and when.

Who can see it

Only you, and the owner of 4D Chicago, who reads the visitor list and bug reports to know who is using the town and what to fix. It is kept with Google Firebase in the United States.

Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. No other student or teacher can see it.

Delete it

In the town, open Settings, then About, and press Delete my saved data. Everything listed above is deleted at once and you are signed out. Signing in again starts a new record.

The sign-in itself (your email with Google Firebase's sign-in service) is removed on request: use the contact page.

Schools

Before a class uses the features that save student work, its school gets our student data agreement. Teachers can ask for it on the contact page.
