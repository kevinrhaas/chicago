01 What this is

Chicago 4D rebuilds the city at set moments in time so you can walk around in it. Every building and road comes from a historical record, and each one shows how sure that record is.

Backstory: a 1965 accident under the Circle campus opened an aperture at Wolf Point, where the river forks. The Apparatus sends you through it. The machine is fiction. The town is real research.

02 Controls

  1. Pick a coordinate under Destination controls. Hover or focus one to target it on the chronometer.
  2. Click or tap it to jump. Esc aborts the jump.
  3. On arrival, choose Jaunts (short guided routes) or explore on your own.
  4. Move with W A S D, sprint with Shift, look with the mouse, inspect a building with E, release the mouse with Esc. On a phone: left thumb moves, drag the right side to look, tap a building to inspect it.
  5. “All coordinates” on the arrival card returns you here.

03 Coordinate status

Lock nominal
Fully built. 1835 is here.
Calibrating
Partly built and still in progress, but cleared for entry. 1904 and 1812 are here. Expect gaps.
Hold
How steady the lock is. It fluctuates, and sometimes slips before the machine pulls it back.

The console’s readings are part of the game. They don’t measure anything in the town.

04 Evidence grades

Attested
A source states it.
Inferred
Reasoned from evidence about that specific thing.
Reconstructed
No source states it, so it is built within stated limits and listed on the Liberties page.

Open any building’s card in the town to see its grade and sources.
