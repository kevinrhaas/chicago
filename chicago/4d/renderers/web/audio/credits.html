Audio library

Recordings, music, sources and liberties for the three eras.

Environmental sounds are modern field recordings used as reconstructed acoustic proxies. They are not recordings of historic Chicago. River and lake recordings are separate; sloughs do not play surf. The owner's supplied score is Suno-generated music, authorized for use in this app. Its 1812 and 1835 collections remain separate.

Each recording names its source format. Most effects currently use Freesound HQ MP3 previews; the night-cricket source is a WAV download. Nature recordings follow their assigned era, habitat, season and time of day, with quiet gaps. Hoofbeats, harness, wagon and work sounds follow their visible sources and use the Effects control. The 1812 snow hoof sound is a damped dirt-road recording, not a recording made on snow. Recordings marked “For review” can be auditioned here but do not yet play in the scene. Sources, edits, habitat references and placement notes are recorded in the catalogue.
