Yard woodpile wood PBR library v1.0.0

Original procedural texture output generated for Kevin Haas's Chicago reconstruction (T-3474)
by tools/generate_yard_wood_pbr.py, which ships with it. No third-party photograph or image
is embedded, sampled or traced in any map.

The maps and generator may be used, modified, and redistributed with the project. Historical
confidence labels and provenance notes should remain attached so reconstructed surfaces are not
silently represented as attested evidence.
