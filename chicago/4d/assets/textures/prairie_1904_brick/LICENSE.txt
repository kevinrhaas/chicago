Prairie Avenue 1904 K03 brick library v1.0.0

Original procedural texture output generated for Kevin Haas's Chicago reconstruction (T-2290)
by tools/generate_prairie_1904_brick.py, which ships with it. No third-party photograph or image
is embedded, sampled or traced in any map.

The maps, profiles.json and the generator may be used, modified, and redistributed with the
project. Historical confidence labels and provenance notes should remain attached so
reconstructed brick is not silently represented as attested evidence.
