Glessner House v4 original PBR material studies

Original procedural output generated for Kevin Haas's Chicago reconstruction by
generate.py, which ships with the maps. No third-party photograph, Google Earth
image, aerial texture, historical photograph or image-generation output is
embedded, sampled, traced or projected in any map.

The maps and generator may be used, modified and redistributed with the project.
Historical confidence labels and provenance notes must remain attached so these
reconstructed surface studies are not silently represented as attested evidence.

Reference photographs were read to understand the house's material families and
relative visual scale. They are not included in this material library. Material
identity and historic form are documented in the structure's source records;
the pixel arrangements, exact colours, noise, aging and course modules here are
reconstructed artistic approximations and are not measured historic surfaces.
