The original GLB is preserved byte-for-byte (SHA256
13600b73c1777545e1454d452c58d141469f031b2c5e6c29b1c6fdefe9145046).
Its artist-authored geometry, UVs and six embedded image payloads are the
licensed basis. The Chicago adaptation normalizes scale and axes, calibrates
body and wheel frames, supplies explicit articulation and reconstructed
interfaces. The exact implemented changes and remaining limits are emitted
by the producer in the versioned sidecar.

The modern model provides no maker, object date, region, prototype identity or
metric calibration. Its use does not attest an1835 Chicago wagon, named owner,
Dearborn type, Studebaker attribution, timber species or period paint.
Appearance/construction adaptation remains reconstructed and must earn actual
matched visual, mechanical, portable LOD and current-world qualification.
