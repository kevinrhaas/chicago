# Older light-skinned male skin, bald crown (T-3441)

MakeHuman's own **old_caucasian_male** system skin (`old_lightskinned_male_diffuse.png`),
from the official `makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip`. CC0 is stated in the material
file's own header (kept here unchanged as `old_caucasian_male.mhmat`). The original
atlas is not kept in the repo: SOURCE.json pins its ZIP entry (size, CRC, SHA-256).

`old_lightskinned_male_bald_crown.png` is that atlas with the painted scalp stubble
blended into the forehead's own tone by `tools/human_skin_bald_scalp.py`, for John J.
Glessner (bald on the crown, white hair at the sides, grown as strands at runtime).
2048 x 2048, the size of the bronze atlas it replaces for his asset, so a phone pays
nothing more for it.

A reusable material component: it does not attest anyone's complexion, health or
likeness. Source category labels ("old", "caucasian") are not person facts.
