# Cleared 2K skin material component (female)

MargaretToigo's **young light-skinned female, bronze variation** (without makeup), from
the official MakeHuman `skins01_cc0.zip` pack ("Natural female skins, shared under CC0").
CC0 is recorded by the official pack metadata (`skins01.original.json`, key
`toigo_light_skin_female_bronze`). The `.mhmat` has no separate license declaration.
The original 2048×2048 RGBA PNG is unchanged; the whole archive was downloaded and
hashed, and the entry's SHA-256 and ZIP CRC checks are in SOURCE.json.

It is the female twin of `skin_toigo_bronze/` (same author, same hm08 UV layout). It
replaces that male atlas on the 1904 working-dress woman (T-3434): the male atlas painted
beard shadow along her jaw and read as a man's face at 3 m.

This is a reusable material component. It does not attest any resident's complexion,
ethnicity, age, health or likeness. Source category labels are not person facts.
Roughness is reconstructed; no normal/roughness images accompany it. See L418.
