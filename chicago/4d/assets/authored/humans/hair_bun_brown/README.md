# Cleared bun hair component (T-3434)

RehmanPolanski's **hair bun brown** (2020), adapted by its author from the MakeHuman
system ponytail01: the hair drawn back smooth from the face to a bun at the back of the
head. It is listed **CC0** in the official MakeHuman `hair01_cc0.zip` pack (pack page and
`packs/hair01.json`, retained here as `hair01.original.json`). The `.obj` and `.mhclo`
headers keep MakeClothes' default export stamp ("license AGPL3 (see also …
external_tools_license)"); the author's own listing in the CC0 pack is the release this
component relies on, and both are recorded in SOURCE.json.

The OBJ, MHCLO, MHMAT and `Hair_bun_brown.png` are the unchanged archive entries, each
checked against the ZIP's CRC-32 (the entries were read by HTTP range from the official
archive; their SHA-256 sums are in SOURCE.json).

`hair_bun_brown_dark_tie.png` is a project derivative (CC0): the source's red velvet
bun tie (the disc at the atlas's lower right) recoloured to a dark hair brown with its
own shading kept, since a working cook of 1904 wore no velvet tie. Nothing else changed.

Fitted by `tools/human_hair_proxy.py` for the 1904 working-dress study (Mattie
Williamson, the Glessners' cook) in place of the men's short04 cap. It is a reusable
reconstructed component and attests no one's hair or likeness (L418).
