Black Hawk War Soldiers who Enrolled at Chicago
Black Hawk War Veterans
who Enlisted at Chicago
These Soldiers are from the Illinois State Archives database which indexes the first volume
of Ellen M. Whitney's "The Black Hawk War 1831-1832". The book was transcribed and donated to the Archives
by Fred Delap of Kansas, Illinois.
From their website: "The Archives will provide copies of the soldier's entry in Whitney's book. For each veteran,
the book's entries generally include name, rank, unit, place and dates of enrollment, and remarks concerning his
service. The information following the rolls includes additional history on the unit and its individuals. As a
result of limitations on staff research time, no more than two names can be searched per request. When making a
request, please include your postal mailing address as well as the soldier's name, unit, place of enrollment, and
war of service.
