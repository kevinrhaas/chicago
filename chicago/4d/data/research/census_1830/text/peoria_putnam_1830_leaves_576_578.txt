1830 FEDERAL CENSUS (FIFTH CENSUS OF THE UNITED STATES)
"PEORIA & PUTNAM COUNTIES & TERRITORY ATTACHED" — THE DISTRICT THAT CONTAINED CHICAGO
NARA M19, Illinois, reel 24 — Internet Archive item populationsc18300024unit, leaves n576 and n578.
Read off the page images themselves on 2026-09-05 for T-0605. The film carries NO text layer (the
item's own OCR metadata says "language not currently OCRable"), so every line below is a hand
reading of the enumerator's hand and nothing else.

WHY THESE TWO LEAVES ARE IN THIS DISTRICT AT ALL. T-0498 declared n576-n579 unread and said they
"belong to the county before this district". That was a guess made from the county column and it
is WRONG for the even leaves. The division's own recapitulation, on n586, prints one age-band row
per page of the division, and those rows are the page totals themselves:

  recapitulation row 1  24/25 34/33 23 15 40/39 28/29 13/20 7/4 4/6   = the totals row of n576,
                        correction for correction (the enumerator struck and rewrote six of the
                        nine cells, and n576's totals row carries the SAME six corrections)
  recapitulation row 2  37 16 29 21 37 26/27 7 9/8 9/4                = the totals row of n578
  recapitulation row 3  20 9 9 2 38 32 11 2 4                         = the totals row of n580
                        (T-0498 read its first five male columns as 20 9 9 2 3; the fifth is 38)
  recapitulation row 5  15 8 8 5 13 4 5 1 1                           = the totals row of n584

So the division is FIVE pages of names — n576, n578, n580, n582, n584, printed pages 299 to 303 —
and n586 (printed page 304) is its recapitulation. n580, where T-0498 began, is page 3 of 5. The
district does not run on past n584: it runs BACK to n576, and these are the two leaves that were
missing from the front of it. See text/peoria_putnam_1830_recapitulation_n586.txt.

THE COUNTY CELL is left empty on both leaves. The heading "Peoria & Putnam Counties & Territory
attached" is written once, on n580, and this project has no evidence that it was written on any
other leaf of the division; the leaves are bound to the district by the recapitulation above and
by nothing else. That is stated so a later reader can weigh it rather than inherit it.

WHERE THESE PEOPLE ARE. n576 opens with Augustus Langworthy, John Hamlin, John L Bogardus and
Abner Eades — the Peoria settlement at the foot of the lake, not the mouth of the Chicago river.
Nothing here is a Chicago residence and nothing here is claimed as one.
