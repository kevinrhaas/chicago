1830 FEDERAL CENSUS (FIFTH CENSUS OF THE UNITED STATES)
"PEORIA & PUTNAM COUNTIES & TERRITORY ATTACHED" — THE DISTRICT THAT CONTAINED CHICAGO
NARA M19, Illinois, reel 24 — Internet Archive item populationsc18300024unit, leaves n580 and n582.
Read off the page images themselves on 2026-09-03 for T-0498. The film carries NO text layer
(the item's own OCR metadata says "language not currently OCRable"), so every line below is a
hand reading of the enumerator's hand and nothing else.

THE COUNTY CELL. On leaf n580 the left-hand column is written once, against the first entry, as
"Peoria & Putnam Counties & Territory attached", and dittoed down. Leaf n582 leaves the cell
empty and continues the same district. The schedule NEVER writes the word "Chicago": there was
no Chicago to write in 1830. What this district is, is the whole of northern Illinois attached
to Peoria and Putnam for enumeration, and the Chicago settlement is the run of households inside
it — Kinzie, Caldwell, Robinson, Beaubien, LaFramboise, Clybourn, Wolcott, Miller, See,
Kercheval, Heacock, Ouilmette and the Fort Dearborn garrison — not a separately headed section.
