WHAT THIS LEAF IS. It carries the same printed schedule head as every other leaf of the division
and NOT ONE head of family. In the names column the enumerator has written five page-total rows
("Total 1st P.", " 2d ", " 3d ", " 4th ", " 5th "), a division total under a rule, and a
"Free White Males / do Females" line beneath that. It is the last leaf of the division, and it is
what proves the names end on n584.

are T-0757's re-reading at full resolution; where it differs from T-0605's the earlier figure is
shown struck:

PAGE 1 IS 55, NOT 53. Both digits of this cell are the SAME glyph, and it is this hand's 5: a
short top bar, a stroke descending from its left end, and a bowl closing to the left at the
baseline. Set beside the 5 of "15" (page-1 male row, fourth cell) it is identical; set beside the
3 of "23" (page-1 male row, third cell) it is not — this hand's 3 is two bumps opening left, with
no descending stroke and no top bar. At w2400 the top bar of the second digit crowds its bowl and
the pair reads 53; at full resolution they separate. The cell therefore AGREES with the 55 heads
read on n576, and page 1 is no longer a disagreement.

PAGE 3 IS 88, AND IT STANDS. Both digits are closed figure-eights. Beside the 38 of the page-3
male row (fifth cell) and the 28 of the page-4 family cell, the second glyph of each of those is
the same closed eight, and this hand's 3 — the one in that same "38" — is plainly the other
shape. The cell is 88 and it is not 38, not 39, and not 89.

THE TOTAL IS 199. The leading figure is a 1 carried in on an approach stroke, which is what made
it look struck through a flourish at w2400; the two figures after it are both this hand's 9 — a
loop closed at the top over a tail that descends and hooks left. 197 is NOT available at this
magnification: the last figure is a 9 and it is formed exactly like the one before it.

WHAT THE ROWS PROVE. Page 1's row carries the SAME six corrections, in the same six cells, as the
totals row of leaf n576. Page 2's row is the totals row of n578. Page 3's is the totals row of
n580. Page 5's is the totals row of n584. The division is therefore n576, n578, n580, n582, n584
(printed pages 299-303) and this leaf; page 4 is n582 by elimination and by its family count of 28,
which is exactly the number of heads read on it.

THE DISCREPANCY, re-measured for T-0757 and STILL NOT RESOLVED. The family column as re-read
sums to 55 + 56 + 88 + 28 + 22 = 249, not to the 199 written under it. The five leaves carry
55 + 56 + 39 + 28 + 22 = 200 heads of family, and both open leaf counts were re-derived for this
ticket off the full-resolution images, independently of T-0498's and T-0605's crops:

  n576   55 name rows, counted head by head in two crops that OVERLAP by one row (Hiram
         Cleaveland closes the first and opens the second), so no row falls in a seam. 55 STANDS.
  n580   39 name rows, counted the same way (John Paylin is the overlapping row). 39 STANDS.

So ONE cell of the five now disagrees with the leaf it totals, not two. Page 1 was the reading
error and it is corrected above: 55 written, 55 read. Page 3 is a real disagreement between the
page and the leaf — 88 written on the recapitulation, 39 heads on n580 — and it is left open.

THE ARITHMETIC OBSERVATION, recorded and NOT adopted. With page 3 taken as 38 the column would
sum to 55 + 56 + 38 + 28 + 22 = 199, which is exactly the total written beneath it, so the
copyist's own total is consistent with a page-3 figure of 38 and with nothing else. That is an
observation about his arithmetic, not a reading: the ink is unambiguously 88 at full resolution
and this project does not correct a page to make a sum work. Note also that 38 would still be one
short of the 39 heads read on n580, so even the reading that closes the copyist's column does not
close it against the leaves.

WHY THAT IS ALLOWED TO STAND. This leaf is headed "A Copy of the —", as n576 and n580 are; it is
a copyist's fair sheet, and its page-4 male row carries "110" and "376" written across their rules
in the same hand. An enumerator's — or a copyist's — arithmetic is allowed to be wrong, and
nothing in this domain is graded on any figure on this leaf. Neither the 88 nor the 39 is
preferred, and no record's confidence moves because of either.
