The Chicago Fire of October 8, 1871 destroyed all county vital
records prior to that date.
Note: The early marriage licenses from Cook County (Chicago), or really
anyplace in Illinois, don't include parents' names ... usually just the name and age of the bride and groom, the
date and place of the marriage (sometimes a church name is given), and the name of the person who performed the
ceremony.
You might have better luck for marriages in the late 1870s to 1890s by contacting the IRAD for the area and ask for the "county clerk register page" instead of a "marriage record" The "record" will just be a piece of paper with groom, bride, date, celebrant and maybe witnesses. The county clerk's REGISTER has much more info on them - birthplaces, # of previous marriages all the witnesses, sometimes parents names - and those are stored in the IL State Archives' satellite offices - IRADs
