# 2013 Prairie, the Reid house: the 2003 nomination photographs (T-3626)

Two of the fifteen photographs in the National Register nomination of the Reid House (NRHP 03000783,
February 2003; NARA naId 28891968), brought in as attributed reference copies, as the owner directed on
2026-11-20 (T-3626, decision c: "Clear the 2003 nomination photographs for use, bring them into the image
store, and read the flanks and rear before closing"). Photographs by Susan M. Baldwin, Baldwin Historic
Properties. Their rights are not established (NARA marks the file "Restricted - Possibly" for copyright), so
nothing is derived from them as an asset: they are read for forms, counts, places and proportions only, and
this credit travels with them. Source record: `data/sources/nrhp_2003_reid_house_nomination.json`.
