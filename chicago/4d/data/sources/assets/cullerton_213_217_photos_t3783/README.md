# 213, 215 and 217 East Twentieth Street — reference copies for the image gate (T-3783)

Four of Jyoti Srivastava's 2009 photographs of the three surviving row houses at 213-217 E. Cullerton Street (East
Twentieth Street before the renaming), the pictures whose cameras T-3783 solved in `data/reference_views/house_213_e20th/`,
`house_215_e20th/` and `house_217_e20th/`. The copies here are the image store's (kevinrhaas/chicago-images,
`prairie-1904/files/<library id>.jpg`), unaltered, brought in with their attribution as the owner directed on 2026-10-11
