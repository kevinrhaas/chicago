# Chicago Democrat, Vol. I No. 1 — 26 November 1833

Page scans of a bound volume, supplied by the project owner on 2026-08-10.
Source record: `data/sources/chicago_democrat_1833_11_26.json`.

These files are deliberately OUTSIDE the published payload — `tools/publish.sh`
copies no part of `data/sources/`, so the 14 MB stays in the repository and out
of the visitor's download, the same rule the GLB masters follow.

`page1.jpg` carries the advertisement columns, including the paper's own imprint
("in the building on the corner of South Water and Clark-streets") and Philo
Carpenter's advertisement of 22 November 1833.

**Read these, not the transcription.** A modern transcription accompanied the
scans and is a finding aid only; it renders the dry-goods firm as "C. & L.
Harmon" where the page plainly reads "C. & I. HARMON". Any claim taken from
this source must be taken from the page image and cited to it.

**The engravings are printers' stock cuts.** The building over "Two Buildings to
Let", the ox, the strayed-cattle cuts: jobbing printers kept generic blocks for
each category of notice and struck the same one for every advertiser. Nothing
about a Chicago building may be read out of them.
