# The 1871 fire landmarks downtown — eleven pictures (T-3688)

Pictures of the Court House, the Chamber of Commerce, the Sherman House, the Tremont House, Crosby's
Opera House, the Palmer House, Field, Leiter & Co.'s store and the Tribune building as they stood before the
fire of 8-9 October 1871, brought in so that each landmark is built against them (the image gate,
`data/reference_views/README.md`). Forms, places and proportions are read from them; no texture or
mesh is derived from any of them. The stereographs are their cards' left halves, cropped from the
holder's scan (and, for the Library of Congress cards, scaled to 1600 or 1400 px wide); the plates are
re-saved page images.
