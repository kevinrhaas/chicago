# Prairie Avenue looking south from 18th Street — reference copy, supplied 2026-10-13

The Alfred Holzman Company postcard "Prairie Avenue", postmarked 1908, supplied by the project
owner on 2026-10-13 with "we want to emulate this" (T-2473, T-2474, T-2475). It is a hand-tinted
print of the negative published as "A Bit of Prairie Avenue" (Flinn, 1891) and "Prairie Avenue"
(Picturesque Chicago, 1892, plate facing p. 199), so it shows the street between Dec. 1887 and
1891. Its source record is `data/sources/picturesque_chicago_1892_prairie_avenue.json`; the
Prairie library lists the postcard as `st-ghm-postcard-1908-prairie-south-at-18th`.

**Rights:** the postcard is **public domain** (a U.S. publication of 1891-1908); the second
photograph's rights are not established (below). Arrangement, counts and proportions
are read from it; nothing is derived from the image as an asset.
