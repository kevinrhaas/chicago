# The 1871 fire places on the North and West Sides — pictures (T-3689)

Pictures of the O'Leary cottages, the Water Tower and the Water Works as they stood just after the fire of 8-9 October
1871, and HABS's modern photograph of the tower, brought in so that each building is built against
them (the image gate, `data/reference_views/README.md`). Forms, places and proportions are read from
them; no texture or mesh is derived from any of them. The stereographs are their cards' left halves,
cropped from the Library of Congress master scans.
