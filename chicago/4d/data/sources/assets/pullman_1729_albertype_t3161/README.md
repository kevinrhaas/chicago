# 1729 Prairie, the George M. Pullman house, 1890 — the Albertype plate, reference copy for the image gate (T-3161)

"Chicago Residences", a collotype plate in *Chicago Illustrated in Albertype* (Albertype Co., New
York, 1890), six vignettes captioned in pairs under each column: Henry J. Sheldon over Potter
Palmer, **Geo. M. Pullman** over D. M. Wells, J. V. Farwell over Archbishop Feehan. The Pullman house
is the top centre vignette (about x 935-1545, y 80-700 px), seen from the south-west across the
Prairie Avenue / 18th Street corner: the Prairie front with its portico on the left, the 18th Street
flank and its arcade on the right. Internet Archive, `chicagoillustrat00albe` (digitised from the
University of Illinois at Chicago's copy, CARLI), page n62; the Prairie library lists it as
`a16-albertype-1890-pullman-residence`, and the copy here is the image store's
(kevinrhaas/chicago-images, `prairie-1904/files/a16-albertype-1890-pullman-residence.jpg`), brought in
with its attribution as the owner directed on 2026-10-11 for the pictures the image gate builds against.

**Rights: public domain** (a US publication of 1890; the Internet Archive: "Public Domain assumed").
Forms, counts, places and proportions are read from it; nothing is derived from the image as an asset,
and the copy is unaltered.
