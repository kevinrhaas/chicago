# Student data agreement (draft) — T-3776

**A draft for the owner, not legal advice.** It follows the points Illinois's Student Online
Personal Protection Act (SOPPA, 105 ILCS 85) asks an operator's agreement with a school to
cover. The owner reviews it, has it checked if he wishes, and sends it to a school before its
classes use 4D Chicago's class features. `docs/STUDENT-DATA.md` is the field-by-field
description it refers to.

---

**Between** the operator of 4D Chicago ("we") **and** _[school or district]_ ("the school").

1. **Purpose.** We provide 4D Chicago, a walkable, source-cited reconstruction of historic
   Chicago, for classroom learning. We use student data only to provide it to the school:
   signing in, saving a student's own work, letting a teacher see work a student hands in, and
   fixing faults.
2. **The data.** The records and fields listed in `docs/STUDENT-DATA.md` (attached), and no
   others. We do not collect grades, scores, location from the device, or contact details beyond
   the sign-in email.
3. **No sale, no advertising, no profiles.** We do not sell or rent student data, use it for
   targeted advertising, or build a profile of a student for any purpose other than the one
   above.
4. **Who has access.** The student; the teacher of a class the student joined, for work the
   student hands in to that class; and the operator, to run the service. Our processor is Google
   (Firebase / Cloud Firestore, United States), under Google's terms for that service.
5. **Security.** Data travels over HTTPS. Access is enforced by Firestore security rules that let
   each student read only their own records and each teacher only their own class.
6. **Breach.** If we learn of unauthorized access to student data, we notify the school within
   30 days of learning of it (or sooner where law requires), with what was affected and what we
   have done.
7. **Deletion.** A student can delete their own saved data at any time (Settings › About ›
   Delete my saved data). The school may ask us to delete any student's data; we do so within
   30 days. A class's records are deleted when its teacher closes it, and in any case at the end
   of the school year it names, unless the school asks otherwise.
8. **Requests.** A parent or student may review or correct the student's data through the
   school, which forwards the request to us; we answer within 30 days.
9. **Changes.** We tell the school before any change to the data we keep, and the switch for
   class features stays off for the school until it accepts the change.
10. **Term.** This agreement lasts for the school year below and renews each year unless either
    side ends it. On ending, we delete the school's class records within 30 days.
