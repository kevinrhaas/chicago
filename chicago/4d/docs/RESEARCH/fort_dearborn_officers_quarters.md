# Fort Dearborn — officers' quarters

> Part of the Fort Dearborn complex. The plan source, the scale derivation, the garrison chronology, the wrong-fort guards and the three corrections to `docs/research/04-structures-south.md` are all in **`docs/RESEARCH/fort_dearborn.md`**, which this memo does not repeat.

## What is evidence

- **Logs, on the west, within the pickets** — Hubbard 1827, standing in the fort. The 1855 key
  says "wood", which is the same claim less precisely.
- **About 30 × 60 ft** — the 1855 key.
- Andreas p. 84: "The officers' quarters were on the west side."

## The one real dimensional disagreement in this complex

The 1855 key gives 30 × 60 ft. The 1830 plan draws the west range about 30 ft deep and about
80 ft long at this project's derived scale — agreement on the depth to 3 %, disagreement on the
length by nearly 40 %. **The stated figure wins**, because the scale that would overrule it was
itself calibrated on the other stated figure in the same caption. The cost is legible: the
modelled building is about 6 m shorter than the plan draws its range. That is why the footprint
is `inferred` and the commandant's is `documented`.

## A correction

`docs/research/04-structures-south.md` § 1.2 reads Andreas as giving brick officers' quarters
here and flags a conflict with the 1855 key. Andreas p. 84 gives no material and Andreas p. 264
gives logs. There is no conflict. See the main memo § 6.
