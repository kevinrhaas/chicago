# Glessner service stairs, rails and rear gate — T-2223

Target: exterior/courtyard, 1904-07-01. The existing north-court flight and
landing keep their plan, ten treads and 1.8288 m landing elevation. They are
separate from the wooden stair behind the stable that HABS sheet 2 dates to
1955; that later stair is excluded. The street and terrace stairs retain the
previous reviewed geometry. The component register distinguishes these sites.

The HABS first-floor plan and section A-A supply the established layout.
Licensed w_lemay photograph 159537058 (October 26, 2024) shows two lower
horizontal rails, sparse posts, brick supports and pale stone capitals.
Photograph 159537051 supplies a closer rail view. These establish surviving
fabric, not the precise 1904 steel stock, joints, finish or date. Modern wrapping,
paint, paving and planting are excluded. Barford originals remain unavailable;
no newly recovered early photograph dates this stair to 1904.

Ten unique posts now sit on actual stone treads and landing surfaces, with
85 mm baseplates. The rail line follows the stair pitch independently of each
post's base elevation. Six continuous bars follow both exposed landing edges with closed, shared miter joints:
a handrail and two lower rails on each exposed side. The retained brick piers
receive bounded chamfered pale capitals within the existing landing height.
Their footings extend below the measured ground, approximately 15 mm below
the structure's local datum. The bottom flight also meets the measured grade. The brick cheek follows each
tread underside; the stone noses have closed overhangs without duplicate top faces.

The rear gate preserves the plan opening at W161.25 ft, S61.3–70.8 ft and its
existing reconstructed 8 ft top. Two boarded leaves replace the plain box;
each has two ledges, a compression brace and two hinges connected to the
masonry reveal. The shared gate reveals alone are dressed stone; the exterior
faces retain their rock finish. The stone sill sits 8.4 mm above the modeled carriage drive, with 12 mm leaf
clearance above it. Terrain alone is 0.44 mm below local datum; the separate
carriage-drive mesh is 36.6 mm above it. Both surfaces are accounted for, so
the threshold is not buried in the drive and the gate does not cut through it.
The sill is 50 mm stone stock, entering grade by 5 mm. The brick cheek
starts at local datum below the raised lawn; separate stone stair/pier
footings extend to -40 mm. The hinge pins stand outside the timber ledges, with straps meeting the
exposed barrels directly. The 45 mm timber, joint depths, hinge and latch stock are bounded replaceable
reconstructions, not an independently sourced 1904 gate elevation.
