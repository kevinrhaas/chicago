This review uses the owner's T-3153 stopping rule. A missing or wrong-shaped/count/material
element, or an independently established displacement over about 0.3 m, is material.
Pixel/camera uncertainty and conflicting picture readings are within tolerance; a feature
an image does not resolve is not shown. No new photo follow-up is filed.

## Camera, dates and the remaining validation

Only one meaningful governing view is enrolled here. It is explicitly diagnostic-only,
so its invented framing is not offered as a historical **From this spot**. Its rays
reproduce the earlier actual Castelnau comparison. The two recovered overcast and
directional-overcast cameras were lighting variants of that same view; neither was
misrepresented as a Kellogg camera.

The completed 1832 tower is distinct from the unfinished tower that fell in 1831.
The latter's fifty-foot height and three-foot walls are not transferred to this model.
The later North Pier light and a 1859 upper bound do not establish the old tower's exact
demolition date or authorize a later tower in 1835. The nominal forty-foot datum remains
unidentified: the reconstructed 12.84968 m top is not independently proved wrong by
subtracting the 12.19 m nominal control. No dated enlargement or fitting change is invented.

No newly established visitor-noticeable defect requires another geometry change or bake.
That conclusion does not silently claim seven independent photographic matches. It records
what all seven pictures resolve, the genuine picture conflicts and what remains unknowable.
