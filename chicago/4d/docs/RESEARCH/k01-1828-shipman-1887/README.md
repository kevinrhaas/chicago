# T-3166 (piece 1 of T-1887) — 1828 Prairie onto K01 from the Shipman photographs

It refines the district draft (T-2329) in place under `house_1828_prairie`. T-3167 (piece 2 of T-1887)
owns 1824 next door; T-3168 (piece 3) owns what the pictures show at 1828 that is not built yet, the
partial view's camera, and the match records' `matches`.

## The pictures

- **The automobile photograph**, undated (Chicagology: "about 1897"; the electric runabout at the
  curb suggests about 1897-1906), the street front square-on from the roadway: source record
  `chicagology_1828_shipman_automobile`, the Prairie library's `cgn-1828-shipman-automobile-copy`
  (and `a18-gh-shipman-1828-with-automobile`, the Glessner House Museum blog's smaller copy of it).
- **The partial view**, undated and damaged, the north part of the front from the north-east with the
  second storey and the mansard: source record `glessner_blog_1828_shipman_partial`, library
  `a18-gh-shipman-1828-partial`.

Both are committed as the image store's copies, with their attribution, at
`data/sources/assets/shipman_1828_photos_t1887/` (rights `check_required`: forms, counts and
proportions are read from them, nothing is derived from them as an asset).

Chicagology's page also transcribes D. B. Shipman's permit (Chicago Tribune, 9 July 1876:
"three-story dwelling, 35x84") and the Inter Ocean's tour ("the light stone-front house, No. 1828").

## What is open (the match record)

The porch's round arches and its third column, its carving and stone balustrade; the stoop's
balustered cheeks; the bay's panelled pilasters; the basement windows' full height (K02's two Lemont
courses hold their heads to 1.05 m); the bay carried up into the mansard with its own dormer (the
partial view); and a stretch of front wall south of the bay that the photograph shows and the 1911
sheet does not.

No generator rule was changed: every part is the K01, K02, K07, K08, K10 and K11 kits as they stand.
