# T-3024 — Glessner's 18th Street stack: its vertical layout

Piece 2 of T-2854. HABS 14's match record held one open item, found by T-2873: the gable's
coping met the stack's sides about 34 px lower in the photograph than in the render, the
1886 stone stood lower under the device's stone (a 13 px gap against 5), and the two crosses
read level here where the record carried photograph 4's stepped reading.

**Acceptance, stated before the work:** each of the three ruled against HABS 14, photograph 4
and HABS 01 (and HABS sheet 6, which draws the stack edge-on), built or argued, Glessner
rebaked, and HABS 14's stack-layout item closed.

`read_layout.py` re-derives every number below into `readings.json` (`--check` compares).
Heights are feet above north grade (ng) unless marked G (the footprint's grade; ng = G - 0.2,
door = G - 0.74).

## The ruling

1. **The coping**: lowered 0.7 ft by taking the parapet from 1.2 to 0.5 ft above its roof. The
   three readings spread from about 0.5 (sheet 6) to 1.5 ft (HABS 14); 0.7 is their middle and
   puts the junction on sheet 6's. It lowers both rakes with it, which HABS 14 also reads.
   The 1888 plate's 1.2 ft (+/- 0.5) is superseded.
2. **The 1886 stone**: 43.3-44.75 ft ng, between the sheet's two anchors and the photograph. Its
   foot barely moves; its head comes down 0.75 ft, leaving a foot of coursing under the device's.
3. **The crosses**: level at the stone's mid-height, 44.05 ft, as HABS 14 reads them.
