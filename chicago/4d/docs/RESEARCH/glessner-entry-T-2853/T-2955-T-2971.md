# Glessner's north grade and front door: the entry's beds raised, the door to 7.50 ft (T-2955, T-2971)

T-2913 (`T-2913.md`) read the courses beside the front door and left two pieces: the
0.20 ft by which the build's granite beds stand under sheet 5's (T-2955), and the door's
height (T-2950). This run did both and re-solved `habs5-entry` on the result; T-2950 was
split on the way (its built piece is T-2971, the rest is T-2972, § 4).

## 1. North grade 0.20 ft over the walk (T-2955)

The Prairie elevation draws the printed course string up from a base line 0.54 ft under the
door sill, 0.20 ft over the walk (sheet 6's -0.74). The 18th Street elevation sets its
apertures on that string's sums above its own grade (L305). One coursing serves both
faces and its beds meet at the NE corner, so its base is one height, and the two sheets
together put the 18th Street grade 0.20 ft over the Prairie walk. `grade_datum` took the
two as level because no sheet prints the offset; it now carries 0.20 ft, `inferred`, with
that reasoning.

Built:

- `grade_datum.north_grade_above_grade_ft` 0.0 -> 0.2. Every height written over north
  grade (sheet 4's: the 18th Street apertures, eaves, ridges, the stacks) rises 0.061 m
  with it, so the apertures stay on their course sums.
- The granite string is laid from north grade (`ashlar_origin_m`), not from the walk. Its
  bottom course runs down to the walk, so the raised origin leaves no uncoursed strip at
  the foot of a wall. Brick coursing and a chimney's own single course keep their origins.
- A tower drum with no `base` stands on the model's grade, as the walls do; the
  north-grade offset no longer lifts the stair tower 6 cm off the court.
- The heightfield is not re-cut: it is flat across the lot to 2 cm, and 6 cm of grade
  difference along 18th Street is inside that.

its head.

## 4. What the true scale shows next (T-2972)

At the scan's true scale the sheet draws the header one course high, from the door head
(7.50) to bed 8 (about 8.75), with a flat top and the fan springing from bed 8. The build
springs the fan at 9.0 and raises the header's centre to 9.3. T-2912 read the fan on the
stretched crop, so its spring (about 8.78 unstretched), stilt (0.5, about 0.35) and crown
all read 3.3 % tall. The header's raised centre comes from the owner's close-up (T-2228)
and has to be argued against the flat-topped drawing. That is T-2972. habs5-entry stays
`differs` on that one item.

Also found, not built here: sheet 6 draws the Prairie basement grilles' heads on bed 4
(4.55 ft over the door datum, the record's 4.7) and the first-floor sills on bed 8 (about
8.8, the record's 9.2). Under the old coursing those sills sat within 0.06 ft of a bed; on
the raised beds they stand 0.14-0.16 ft under one. Filed as its own ticket.
