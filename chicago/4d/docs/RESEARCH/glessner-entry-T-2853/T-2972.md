## The raised centre

The sheet does not draw the header flat everywhere. Its top is flat to the shoulders and rises
in a shallow segment under the tympanum, about 0.14 ft at the centre, and the tympanum's legs
stand on that curve. The owner's close-up (T-2228) shows a raised centre, and that photograph
is taken at an angle with the door set behind the arch. So the two agree on a raised centre,
and the sheet, which is square to the wall, gives its height and width. The office's doorway
No. 23A simplifies this edge. Its source record already says the built HABS detail controls
the outline. The header is now built that way: flat shoulders on bed 8, and a circular
segment of 5 ft chord and 0.14 ft rise (`prairie_entry.lintel_crown_width_ft`).
