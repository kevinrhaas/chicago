# Glessner's Prairie fan, stilted to HABS sheet 5 (T-2912, a piece of T-2853)

T-2853 asked for the front entry brought to HABS sheet 5 (`habs5-entry`): the arch's
proportion, the stone joints, the door leaf and hardware, and the carving read at the
drawing's grain. Reading the sheet split it in two. This piece (T-2912) is the arch, its
joints, the field's carving and a ruling on the door; the wall's courses and the header
stone beside the door are T-2913.

All picks are on `../glessner-entry-2228/habs5-entry.png`, the rectified crop the camera
is solved on: 100 px a foot both ways, the door 5 ft wide (x 642-1142) and 7.75 ft high
(y 938-1713), so the door's centre line is x 881-892 and its head y 938. Each pick is
about ±5 px (±0.05 ft).

## 1. The head is stilted

| | across (half) | over the spring line (y 808) | centre over the spring |
|---|---|---|---|
| the fan's extrados | x 232-1527, 6.48 ft | top y 102, 7.06 ft | 0.58 ft |
| the tympanum's dark ring | x 630-1132, 2.51 ft | top y 510, 2.93 ft | 0.47 ft |

Both rings are taller than they are half-wide, by the same half foot, and the drawing runs
each one plumb down from its widest point to the spring line. The build had them as
semicircles on the spring (the extrados 6.4 ft, its crown at 15.4 ft against the sheet's
16.06). The Richardson office's doorway No. 23A (`a18-rba-mf-glessner-f29-entrance-detail-ink`,
AIC 69665) draws the same stilt, about 0.08 of the radius, and the period photograph
`a18-gh-glessner-front-with-keith` shows the tympanum's sides standing plumb on the lintel.
T-2228's review had read this as "a somewhat taller drawn arch proportion" and declined to
force the radius; the radius was right, the centre was not.

Built: `stilt_ft: 0.5`, `ring_ft: 6.5`, the spring unchanged at 9.0 ft. The wall's cut, the
fifteen voussoirs (the two springers run plumb to the spring), the carving bed, the billet
channel (with its billets carried down at their pitch), the archivolt floor and every roll
follow the stilt. Re-rendered, the fan's crown is within 0.06 ft of the sheet's and the
tympanum ring's about 0.1 ft high: one stilt serves both rings, between the sheet's 0.47
and 0.58.

## 2. The field: eight rings, not a mirrored tree

At the sheet's grain the field is stems bent into rings, each curling inward, unequal and
not mirrored, rising from a small boss at the foot. The build had a straight trunk with
two mirrored pairs of roundels and a crowning one, laid out by eye from the owner's
photographs (L-glessner-stone-faces-2416, whose "How to resolve" asks for a measured
drawing of the tympanum to replace the carving's sizes and counts). No. 23A sketches the
same scrolling. Read, feet from the door's centre line and over the spring line:

`masonry_house_v4_ornament.FIELD_ROUNDELS` holds them in shares of the field's radius

keep the owner's photographs' leafy rosettes inside them: the sheet's stipple does not
resolve a leaf, so the leaves stay reconstructed carving.

## 3. The door leaf on the sheet is a later one

The sheet's leaf has a seven-by-seven grille with spear tips and no figures, no strap, a
knob at the left stile and one lower panel. The built leaf is the office's sheet No. 95
(T-2216): six bars each way to round knobs, 1800 in the grille, a strap with a central
boss, two panels. The period photograph decides it: contrast-stretched, it shows 1800
about a fifth of the way down the leaf and a knob at mid-height on the leaf's centre line,
which is where sheet No. 95 puts the number row and the strap's boss, and the 1963 leaf
has neither. So the sheet measured a later leaf; the record keeps sheet No. 95, and the
match record marks the leaf `not shown` (this picture cannot show the 1904 door).

## 4. Left for T-2913

The sheet's course lines either side of the door fall at about 7.1, 5.8, 4.7, 3.8, 2.5
and 1.0 ft over the door datum; the build's at 7.3, 6.7, 6.1, 5.5, 4.4, 3.6, 2.3 and 0.8.
The sheet's header stone has joints at about S 27.7 and 36.0; the build's 10 ft stone is
the owner's photographs', and the period photograph shows a stone of about 10.6 ft. That
is a reading of the whole entry wall and a ruling between the two, and it is T-2913's.
