- **asserted** — the printing IS a word in the residents vocabulary.
- **folded** — a synonym ruled onto a controlled word (`boot and shoe maker` ->
  `shoemaker`, `counsellor at law` -> `attorney`).
- **refused** — a printing deliberately given no controlled word: one that names
  TWO trades (`watchmaker and jeweller`), or one the vocabulary has no word for
  (`ventriloquist`, `capitalist`, `botanic practitioner`).

  the compound directory lines that carry a trade, an address and a firm in one

A refused or unresolved row is NOT a dropped row. Every one of them is on the card
as a dated role carrying its printing, its bound and its source; what it does not

does not list — Peter Cohen, Ira Couch, John Murphy and William Walters. **The

would make the residency appear to rest on a volume that never addressed it. The
role says so instead, in its own note, and the validator's gate is unchanged: at
