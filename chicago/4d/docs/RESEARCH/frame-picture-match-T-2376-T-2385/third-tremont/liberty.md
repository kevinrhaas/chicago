### First Tremont opaque arch and plain entry outlines (T-2385 follow-up)

**Covers:** `tremont_house_1.frame_1833.form.gable_arch_treatment`, `tremont_house_1.frame_1833.form.corner_entry_surround`.

Re-inspection of the owner-approved attributed Gifford/Chicagology reproduction shows irregular light lettering-like marks within the dark gable arch, rather than a clearly resolved radial fanlight pattern. `gable_arch_treatment=opaque_panel` is the cautious working alternative: keep the prior shallow outline but use an opaque matte warm-charcoal field and remove invented radial bars/glass. It does **not** invent readable letters, a hotel-name inscription, original pigment or exact construction. Lettering, sign-versus-window identity and exact feature date remain unresolved. The opposite hidden arch remains a repeated reconstruction. Default `fanlight` preserves all earlier non-opted behavior exactly.

`corner_entry_surround=true` adds a plain broad jamb/header outline to the existing entries. Source occurrence is visible, but stock width0.14m, depth65mm, existing trim tone, joinery and the mirrored concealed entries are reconstructed. The existing leaf/opening, raised landing, floor envelope, footprint/position, roof controls and source-date limits stay unchanged. Neither a classical order, a pediment nor ornamental lettering is claimed.

Source `gifford_tremont_house_no_1`: N. Roswell Gifford, reproduced by Chicagology, https://chicagology.com/prefire/prefire021/.
