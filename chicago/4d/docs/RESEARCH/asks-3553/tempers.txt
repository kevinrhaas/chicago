# Temperaments for "Ask a question" and "Tell me a story" (T-3553). The owner, 2026-11-16:
# "Give everyone a unique personality as well their words and perspectives are driven from their
# history ... ideally the kinds of perspectives and language and story would be related to the
# kinds of things they would discuss with you."
#
# tools/build_tales.py gives every figure one temperament, weighted by who their record says they
# are (a soldier on the eve of the march leans wary, an Irish labourer wry, a land man shrewd), and
# two or three things they like to talk about, from their record first. Both steer which questions
# a person offers you and which stories they tell. A temperament's lines are how they come to an
# answer, said of them, never put in their mouth: {he} {his} {him} are the teller's pronouns.
#
# `topics:` the things this temper drifts toward. Topic codes (build_tales.py TOPICS):
#   trade  money, land, business, prices      kin    children, wives, husbands, family
#   home   the place left behind, letters     faith  church, prayer, the spirits
#   war    soldiers, the fort, fighting       wild   lake, river, prairie, beasts, hunting
#   work   a trade, a day's labour            fun    music, dancing, games, a joke
#   talk   news, politics, other people       past   old days, what used to be
#   dread  fear, sickness, fire, danger
