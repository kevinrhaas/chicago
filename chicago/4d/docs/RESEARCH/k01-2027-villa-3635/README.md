## The design (no picture)

No photograph, plate or description of 2027 is held, so the house is a bounded design on the T-1917 brief
('restrained roof and openings with separate service elevation'): a brown pressed-brick front over dun
commons, K09 cornice-hood lintels, a K10 bracketed cornice carried round the full-height front bay, a tin hip
at 38 degrees with one street dormer for the half storey (the kit's slate is cut for steeper roofs), a
two-storey canted bay on the north flank under its own low hip, the door at the head of a 1.6 m K07 stoop at
the front's south corner where the sheet draws the stoop, and the rear range under a flat tin deck. The south
flank is blank: 2029's wall stands about 0.4 m off it.
