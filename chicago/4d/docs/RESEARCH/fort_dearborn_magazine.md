# Fort Dearborn — powder magazine

> Part of the Fort Dearborn complex. The plan source, the scale derivation, the garrison chronology, the wrong-fort guards and the three corrections to `docs/research/04-structures-south.md` are all in **`docs/RESEARCH/fort_dearborn.md`**, which this memo does not repeat.

## What is evidence

- **"The magazine, of brick, was situated about half way between the west end of the guard and
  block-houses"** — Hubbard 1827. Andreas p. 84 lists a magazine among what the stockade
  enclosed.

That is the whole of it. **The 1830 plan does not draw it** — either it was too small for the
plate or it was gone by 1830, and nothing decides between those.

## The position is the best-attested thing about it and the least certain in metres

"About half way" is the entire fix, and the sentence parses two ways — midway between the two
buildings' west ends, or midway between the guard house's west end and the block-house — which
move the point about 4 m apart. It is graded `inferred` because a stated relation between two
located buildings is an inference and not a guess; everything else about this building is
`conjectural`.

## A tempting parallel that is refused

The 1803 War Department instruction for posts of this class specified "the magazines for powder
to be of brick of a conic figure". That is the specification for the **first** fort. It is not
evidence that this magazine was conical, and it is built as a plain gabled block.
