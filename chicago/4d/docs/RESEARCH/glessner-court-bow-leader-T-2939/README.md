The pipe's 0.10 m is the narrow side of the 160 x 100 mm stock T-2221 took from the modern courtyard close-up, so the
sheet and that photograph agree once the leader stands on the north range's face with its broad fluted face to the
south; it had stood on the bow wall with that face to the west, where sheet 4 would have drawn it 0.16 m.

The two circles are drawn in the same thin line as the brick coursing, not dotted as hidden work, and lie inside the
head's outline, so they are on its side. The drawing does not say what they are; they are built as round bosses.
