# Fort Dearborn — soldiers' barracks

> Part of the Fort Dearborn complex. The plan source, the scale derivation, the garrison chronology, the wrong-fort guards and the three corrections to `docs/research/04-structures-south.md` are all in **`docs/RESEARCH/fort_dearborn.md`**, which this memo does not repeat.

## What is evidence

- **On the east side of the inclosure** — Hubbard 1827 and Andreas p. 84, independently. The
  1830 plan draws its longest range there, with the sutler's north of it, which is exactly
  where Hubbard's sentence puts the sutler's relative to the barracks.

## The fire

Hubbard's account continues past the description: after the September 1827 annuity payment
"these quarters were struck by lightning and totally consumed, together with the store-house and
a portion of the guard-house". The 1830 plan draws a range on the east side three years later,
so the barracks was rebuilt or replaced between those dates. **That is the only evidence this
project has that any of the fabric standing in 1835 is newer than 1816**, and nothing describes
the rebuilding.

## What is ours

The size (about 90 × 36 ft off the plan, ±20 %), the storey count, the wall height, the roof,
the three stacks and the plain front — **L42**. Read the depth with more suspicion than the
length: on the plate the depth is a seventeen-pixel measurement with a three-pixel line.
