## Method and limits

The frozen `pass_09_75_cohort.json` membership was not changed. Each person received exact-name and reasonable variant/order searches against the repository corpus and open-web historical sources, emphasizing Chicago/Cook County and the surrounding Great Lakes migration corridor. Search normalization was used as a query aid only.

A Chicago post-office letter-list appearance establishes postal reachability, not a residence address or physical presence on July 1, 1835. Same-name matches without date/place or another discriminator were not promoted. Competing geography, chronology conflicts, and OCR variants remain candidates. Negative searches are documented outcomes, not proof of nonexistence.

The human-reviewable workbook and CSV under `chicago/reference/resident-research/T-0483/` retain per-person queries, source IDs/URLs, confidence, supporting/contradicting evidence, limitations, and recommended downstream actions. No canonical resident/household fields are changed by this pass.
