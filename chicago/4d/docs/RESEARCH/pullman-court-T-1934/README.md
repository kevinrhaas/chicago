# Pullman court, T-1934 (2026-11-02)

The court between the palm house's terrace and 18th Street, read through Taylor's 16555 (its camera
re-solved on the 18th Street wall's piers) and the 1894 Inland plate, as sent to the owner before it was
built: `court-plan-levels.png` (top-down, every level in metres over the court's ground) and
`court-plan-evidence.jpg` (the plan as an isometric and overlaid on both pictures).

Built as drawn, except the striped awning: the plan put it over the terrace's south front; read again
through the re-solved camera it is a lean-to off the house's east wall, its south end at v -12.5 (an eave
at 4.2 m over the terrace, which the front placing would have left 1.6 m over the floor), with a striped
side curtain down that end. The record (`data/structures/pullman_house_1729_prairie_conservatory.json`,
`host_wall.court`) and liberty L-pullman-court-1934 are the authority.
