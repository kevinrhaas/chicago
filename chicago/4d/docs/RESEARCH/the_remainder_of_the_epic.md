# The remainder of the epic — the written ruling on the last 1,094 unasserted units

*T-1298, the third and last child of T-1236. Its siblings are T-1296 (land_sales, 1,572
units, [a sale is never a residence](a_sale_is_never_a_residence.md)) and T-1297
(civic, census_1830 and directories, 718 units).*

## What was unanswered

The closed research-spend ledger (T-1143) gives every registered reading unit a durable
outcome. `unresolved` is the one label it has for a reading it cannot place, and a unit
may only be unresolved while it is **owned by live work**. T-1236 owned 3,384 of them by
default; split by weight into three children, 1,094 came here:

| Domain | Units | What they are |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| residents | 382 | rows of the pilot and pass 02–15 cohort manifests whose pass finding names no exact source-bearing structured resident field |
| newspapers | 378 | the person, notice, event, shipping and price units of the thirteen held issues that no card names |
| church | 272 | register entries outside the later-only and outside-Chicago dispositions the readings already carry |
| books | 61 | the non-person readings — ground, harbour, weather, price, shipping, institution, household |
| genealogytrails | 1 | one landscape reading |

None of them was doubted. They were unanswered because the readings say nothing about
*themselves* that the ledger's derivations can read: a cohort row carries no date, a
newspaper unit carries no `describes_date` at all (its date is the masthead, in the
issue file), a register entry is dated but says nothing about what its role means for
1835, and a reading of the price of flour names nobody to attach to.

## How they were answered

Five **derived** ruling registers, at `data/research/<domain>/spend_rulings.json`, on the
terms `research_spend_ledger.ruling_registers` sets — the same statement floor, the same
note floor, the same coverage faults as the hand-authored register T-1234 wrote. They are
generated by `tools/spend_remainder_rulings.py` and re-derived by its `--check` in the
gate, because 1,094 typed notes would drift and nobody could then prove a note still
matched the row it claims to rule. Every note is built from the unit's **own** committed
fields, so a reader can set the note beside the row and see it says what the row says.

The corpus the registers must cover is fixed by `natural_disposition` — the derivation
that reads no ruling register at all — so writing them cannot change what they are asked
to cover.

## The sixteen rules

**Closed — 536 units.** A closed unit is a finished answer, not a shelved one.

- `the_manifest_is_a_reservation_and_not_a_reading` (75) — the pilot manifest's own
  preamble: *"a sampling manifest, not new evidence about any person."* No findings
  ledger was ever committed for the pilot, so the row has no reading attached to spend.
  What is refused is the claim that the ROW carries an 1835 fact; the person's card is
  untouched.
- `the_pass_named_a_candidate_and_did_not_assert_it` (162) — the finding's own candidate
  rows carry `asserted: false` and name the conflict that stopped them. The research
  already refused the join; the ledger now carries that refusal.
- `corroboration_confirms_and_moves_nothing` (47) — outcome `corroborated`: *confirmed,
  not moved.* Corroboration corroborates; it names no new structured field.
- `the_reading_is_earlier_than_the_scene_and_does_not_reach_it` (34) — a man, a society
  or a house at Chicago in 1827 is not thereby at Chicago in 1835.
- `the_issue_is_printed_after_the_scene_date` (51) and
  `the_roll_is_beyond_the_reading_window` (26) — T-0513's ladder, applied from the
  masthead date and from the entry's own `beyond_ticket_window` field.

**Spent in aggregate — 141 units.** True, in the window, attached to no record.

- `the_market_and_the_port_in_aggregate` (55 newspapers, 5 books) — this is the question
  the ticket asked: what a reading of the harbour or the price of flour does for the 1835

  the person ledger has nothing to write from it, and the vessels and stocks it counts

## What this does not decide

**The letter-list question is not answered here.** Whether a name whose whole evidence is
a letter waiting at the Chicago post office belongs in 1835 is T-0660 → T-0691, blocked
on the owner, and its outcome is not invented. What is ruled is the only thing that can
be ruled without it: the name was read and it is withheld, and T-1159's field is exactly
that roster — read-and-withheld names with their source, reason and re-admission class,
of which `letter-list-only` is one the ticket names. Recording a withholding is not
ruling on a residency.

Nothing here edits a resident, mints a person, moves a confidence, invents a citation or
re-adjudicates an identity.
