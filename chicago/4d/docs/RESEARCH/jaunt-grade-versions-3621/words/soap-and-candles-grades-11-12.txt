== premise
Survey from Wolf Point what Daniel Elston and Company's works up the North Branch manufactured, compose a concise list on South Water Street, determine what to carry, and convey it home to a boarding house behind Peck's store.

== opening
JAUNT: SOAP AND CANDLES. Dusk arrives progressively earlier, because autumn signals that the prolonged darkness is approaching: once the lake freezes, "during the long six months of winter the inhabitants, were cut off from the rest of the world". Illumination and soap are what sustain a crowded boarding house through those isolated months.

You board in the log house behind Peck's store. Survey the North Branch toward the soap and candle works, compose a concise list on South Water Street, determine what to carry, and convey it home before complete darkness descends.

== stop the-works
Commence at Wolf Point and survey the North Branch to its eastern bank, where the scene positions Daniel Elston and Company's works, although no source establishes the actual location. Their notice, printed during 1833 and 1834, advertised hard and soft soap, wax and other candles, and offered cash for tallow and household ashes. Every family's kitchen fat and fireplace ashes return transformed into soap and illumination.

== narrative the-works
Tallow and ashes enter the works, while soap and illumination emerge for the town.

== label the-works soap-first

== stop the-list
Across the river to Jones's grocery on South Water Street. His November 1833 notice advertised sugar and most varieties of provisions at the lowest prices, although whether he stocked soap or candles it does not specify, and only the street itself constitutes evidence. Candles mean fire, and the town comprehends the hazard: since 1833 every stovepipe penetrating a roof must be "guarded by tin or sheet iron".

== narrative the-list
Compose the list methodically, and determine what you will carry home.

== label the-list candles
Carry candles home

== consequence the-list candles
A bundle of candles, individually dipped in tallow and secured together with string.

== label the-list soap
Carry a cake of hard soap

== consequence the-list soap
One substantial cake of hard soap, practically as weighty as a building brick.

== label the-list both
Carry some of each

== consequence the-list both
A considerably heavier armful, encompassing the entire list.

== stop lake-street
Proceed to Lake Street and the two-story frame store Thomas Church constructed, reputedly the first store building on the street. Church arrived in 1834, the year business "began to extend to Lake street". A single undated sentence constitutes the entirety of the evidence, and because it identifies no merchandise, nothing is purchased here.

== narrative lake-street
Redistribute the bundle in your arms and inventory what you are carrying.

== stop home
Return to the log house behind Peck's store, which Andreas identifies as Mrs Rufus Brown's, one of the first-class boarding houses. In 1834, he writes, such establishments were perpetually full, occasionally accommodating three in a bed. Nevertheless, Moses and Kirkland maintain the snowbound months were "perhaps, the most enjoyable of the entire year".

== narrative home
A solitary candle illuminates a considerable amount in a crowded room.

== leg 0
With the priority between soap and illumination determined, the list follows.

== leg 2
The concluding steps homeward with the bundle balanced in your arms.

== ending an-armful
Soap and candles both, conveyed home to the log house behind Peck's, so the entire list is complete, whichever item occupied the top position. Let the interminable winter arrive.

== ending candle-lit
Candles at the top of the list, and candles conveyed home, so tonight the crowded room behind Peck's possesses illumination for reading and mending, and the darkness arrives somewhat later.

== ending washing-day
With soap prioritized at the top of the list and successfully conveyed home, the accumulated laundry need wait no longer.

== ending the-other-errand
You departed intending to purchase one item and conveyed home the alternative, which will be required soon enough, and the list will endure until next time.
