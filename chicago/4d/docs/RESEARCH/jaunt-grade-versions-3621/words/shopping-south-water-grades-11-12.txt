== premise
Complete a modest household list along the commercial riverfront without succumbing to every temptation on display.

== opening
JAUNT: SHOPPING SOUTH WATER STREET. This is the settlement's principal thoroughfare, and it is hardly picturesque: the river on one side, plank walks and mud on the other, and every establishment crammed with merchandise unloaded from the vessels. In 1833 the Democrat quoted tea at 87 cents to $1.25 a pound and calicos "from 15 to 38 cents" a yard.

You possess three dollars and a concise household list: something for a cough, a staple and tea. Three establishments on South Water supply them, and you will reconcile your purchases on Lake Street, where the town's commerce is migrating.

== stop remedy
Commence at Philo Carpenter's, the druggist at the Sign of the Golden Mortar. He disembarked in 1832 while cholera afflicted the fort, dispensed his drugs from a log house beside the river, and constructed this store the following summer. He also "prepared and circulated the first temperance pledge" in town, combining commerce with moral reform. His June notice enumerates aloes, ipecac, liquorice ball and tooth powder.

== narrative remedy
Purchase the remedy immediately, or trust that the household's cough eventually subsides independently.

== label remedy liquorice
Liquorice ball for a cough — 25¢

== consequence remedy liquorice
A small paper of it is deposited in the basket alongside the household list.

== stop staples
A short distance east, Peck's store occupies the corner of LaSalle and South Water. His 1833 advertisement enumerates dry goods, hardware, groceries and crockery, with fifty barrels of superfine flour in stock, and an 1834 notice supplements the inventory with flannels, ginghams and calicoes.

== narrative staples
Select the single staple the household requires most urgently, or conserve the purse.

== label staples flour
Flour for the bin — $2

== consequence staples flour
The indispensable staple, consuming the majority of the purse.

== label staples calico
A length of calico — $1.25

== consequence staples calico
Cloth for mending, while the bread awaits a subsequent trip.

== stop compare
Further east at Clark Street stands Harmon, Loomis and Company, whose June 1835 notice offers Young Hyson and Souchong tea, loaf sugar, crockery and glassware. Andreas specifies the intersection of Clark and South Water, although not which corner. Their business is prospering: next year the firm will construct "the Harmon and Loomis block of four-story brick stores on South Water street".

== narrative compare
Compare the merchandise on the shelf with the funds remaining, and decide accordingly.

== label compare hyson
Young Hyson tea and loaf sugar — $1

== consequence compare hyson
The concluding item on the list, accompanied by sugar.

== label compare souchong
A smaller paper of Souchong — 40¢

== consequence compare souchong
The purse is insufficient, so a more economical tea substitutes for the Hyson.

== label compare bowl
A crockery bowl instead — 50¢

== consequence compare bowl
Not on the list, but the household's bowl is cracked, so the tea is postponed.

== stop receipt
Drive south along Clark to Lake Street, where in 1834, Moses and Kirkland write, business "began to extend to Lake street", and Thomas Church was among that year's arrivals. A modern note credits him with the first store building on this street, a two-story frame, although it specifies no merchandise, so nothing is purchased here. Reconcile your basket meticulously.

== narrative receipt
Calculate what the basket contains and what the purse retains.

== leg 0
With the cough addressed, select the essential staple the household requires most urgently.

== leg 1
Tea constitutes the final item on the list, and the purse is considerably depleted.

== leg 2
With nothing further to purchase, locate a quiet spot away from the river and reconcile the expenditures.

== ending list-filled
A staple, a remedy and tea: with the list completed and a few coins remaining, the household is provisioned for this week, and you comprehend South Water Street better than most newcomers.

== ending list-short
A portion of the list must await another expedition, so you return home with whatever the purse permitted, and South Water Street, mud and all, will endure.

== keepsake text
A fictional household list and receipt from three South Water Street establishments, reconciled on Lake Street.
