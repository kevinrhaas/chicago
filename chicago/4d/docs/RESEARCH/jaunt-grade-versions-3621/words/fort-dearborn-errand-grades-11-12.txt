== premise
Convey a modest supply requisition around the fort's everyday service buildings and out to its workshop.

== opening
JAUNT: FORT DEARBORN ERRAND. A winter morning at the river mouth, with the lake an iron grey and navigation suspended for the season, and one settler recalled that "There was nothing coming in, and there was no means of sending anything out of the town". Beyond the settlement, on the prairie, "Wolves were in great plenty all around the town". Within the pickets, the garrison administers its stores.

You carry a requisition: select a small supply at the sutler's, verify the parcel against the note at the store-house, and deliver it to the fort's workshop beside the lake.

== stop errand
This is the fort's south gate, garrisoned by federal troops since June 1832. Juliette Kinzie, who resided within in 1831, described high pickets and substantial gates to the north and south, and Hubbard located the guard-house and store-house flanking this gate in 1827. In a frozen season, whatever the stores contain constitutes the entire supply until spring reopens the lake to shipping.

== narrative errand
State your errand to the sentry and proceed across the parade ground to the sutler's.

== stop supply
East of the north gate stands the sutler's store, the post trader's establishment, which supplied whatever the official military ration omitted. Hubbard situated it here in 1827, and an 1855 photograph key still identifies it as the Sutler's, although its proprietor in 1835 remains undocumented.

== narrative supply
Select one supply for the requisition, or proceed without purchasing anything.

== label supply candles
A bundle of candles

== consequence supply candles
Illumination for abbreviated winter evenings at a workbench.

== label supply thread
Thread and a paper of needles

== consequence supply thread
Compact and lightweight, yet perpetually scarce somewhere within the garrison.

== stop account
East of the south gate is the store-house, which the Army's 1834 beef contracts designate the subsistence store house: fresh beef arrived in quarters, up to three times weekly, probably totaling 400 pounds each week. Verify your parcel against the requisition before proceeding further.

== narrative account
Account for every item in the parcel before it departs the pickets.

== label account tally
Tally the parcel against the note

== consequence account tally
Everything the requisition specified is present, and you record it accordingly.

== stop deliver
Outside the pickets toward the lake, the 1830 plan depicts a building labeled Shop. Because no trade is specified, this was presumably a general workshop rather than a smithy. Conceivably it was here that Jeremiah Porter, "the pioneer Protestant minister of Chicago", "preached a sermon in the carpenter's shop of the garrison on the 19th of May, 1833", an improvised sanctuary characteristic of the frontier. Set the parcel down.

== narrative deliver
Deliver what the requisition specified, or return with whatever is missing.

== label deliver hand-over
Set the tallied parcel down at the shop

== consequence deliver hand-over
The errand is complete, and the requisition returns folded in your pocket.

== leg 0
The first line of the requisition: one small supply from the sutler's.

== leg 1
Before the parcel departs the fort, verify it against the requisition.

== leg 2
The final line of the requisition: convey the parcel to the workshop.

== ending accounted
Supply selected, parcel verified and delivered to the workshop. The fort functions as a working neighbor at the river mouth, with stores to administer and an extended winter through which to administer them.

== ending unfinished
A portion of the requisition returns with you. The fort's service buildings will remain tomorrow, along with the remainder of the list, and the inevitable snow.

== keepsake text
A fictional supply requisition, verified outside Fort Dearborn's store-house and conveyed to the workshop on the outer ground.
