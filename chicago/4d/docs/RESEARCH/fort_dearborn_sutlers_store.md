# Fort Dearborn — sutler's store

> Part of the Fort Dearborn complex. The plan source, the scale derivation, the garrison chronology, the wrong-fort guards and the three corrections to `docs/research/04-structures-south.md` are all in **`docs/RESEARCH/fort_dearborn.md`**, which this memo does not repeat.

## What is evidence

- **"East of the north gate, and north of the soldiers' barracks"** — Hubbard 1827. It is a
  two-way fix, and the 1830 plan satisfies both halves of it: a short range against the north
  wall, east of the break in it, with the long east range below.
- The 1855 photograph key letters "D is the Sutler's".

## An alternative identification, unresolved

Robert Fergus, of the fort in 1850, mentions "another building in the Fort enclosure … just
east of the block-house" which "was the quartermaster's or sutler's quarters" — his own "or".
That building is at the other end of the fort from this one. Either it is a second building this
project does not model, or it is a confused memory. **No quartermaster's quarters is built**,
because a source that cannot decide between two names is not evidence for either.

The size off the plan, the storey count, the wall height, the roof and the stack.
