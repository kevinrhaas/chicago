# Fort Dearborn — commandant's quarters

> Part of the Fort Dearborn complex. The plan source, the scale derivation, the garrison chronology, the wrong-fort guards and the three corrections to `docs/research/04-structures-south.md` are all in **`docs/RESEARCH/fort_dearborn.md`**, which this memo does not repeat.

## What is evidence

- **Brick, twice.** Hubbard 1827: "the brick building, just within the north stockade previously
  occupied by the commanding officers". The 1855 photograph key: "the Commandant's Quarters, A
  (brick, about 25×50 ft.)".
- **Its size, once** — and it is the only stated dimension of any building in this fort.
- **Its place**: inside the north wall, west of the north gate (Hubbard; the plan draws the
  range there).
- **At least two chimneys**, implied by the 1855 caption locating the ferry landing "under the
  west chimney of the Commandant's quarters".

## Why this one building matters beyond itself

Its 50 ft length is the calibration for the whole complex: the plan draws the range 48 pixels
long, and 48 px = 50 ft is where the 1.10 ft/px scale comes from. The checks that the
calibration is not circular are in the main memo § 3.

## What is ours

Storey count (inferred from size and prominence), wall height, roof form and pitch, and where
the two stacks stand.
