# Horse and tack: first production master handoff

T-2799 research/specification deliverable, 2026-10-22. This document and the adjacent machine-readable specification define work for T-2800–T-2805. They do not qualify a photographic horse, fit, motion, rider or scene placement. The source register separates inspected pixels from bibliographic leads and historical use from modern anatomy.

## Decision and evidence boundary

Build one original, medium adult light-harness horse first, using cleared anatomical scholarship and CC0 living-animal references. Retain the existing prototype as a technical regression fixture; its coarse silhouette is not the production body. No individual Chicago horse, breed, sex, owner, coat, marking or measured historical dimension is attested. The proposed body identifier is `equine_light_harness_reconstructed_01`; the current fixture is `equine_light_harness_skinned_prototype_01` and must not silently acquire a production label.

The first body is a reconstruction of animal anatomy, not a copy of the modern Konik's breed or the other modern horse's blaze/stockings. Reference photographs supply soft-tissue continuity, eyelid/muzzle/hoof surfaces and hair direction. Seton and Ellenberger/Baum/ Dittrich supply anatomical relationships, with publication/edition limits in their source records. They do not supply a calibrated cutting surface. The 1.55 m withers target is a disclosed engineering scale choice; a missing archival measurement does not prohibit an honest reconstruction.

The first detailed driving-gear basis is the directly inspected John Moore 1891 No.1 light buggy-harness text, for a before-1904 American commercial family. It is separate from the same catalog's English coach collar/hames family and from riding saddlery. Its use on an 1812 or 1835 horse is not established. An earlier horse body can be reconstructed from general anatomy, but an earlier harness/saddle needs its own period rationale and fit before scene placement.

The BlueMesh draft-horse lead has a saved CC BY 4.0 declaration and a strong preview, but the normal model-download endpoint returned 401. No mesh was acquired; thumbnail quality is not package/scale/rig/texture verification. No access controls are bypassed. This acquisition lead is optional; the original anatomy rebuild can proceed now.

## Current implementation, measured from committed bytes

The adjacent JSON pins input hashes and the actual inventory snapshot. The horse GLB is 715,316 bytes, 14,172 triangles, six material draws and a 15-joint skin. It contains synthetic in-place idle, walk and trot clips; no embedded images or texture maps and no TEXCOORD attributes. The body's current rest-frame accessor bounds are approximately X −0.32..0.32 m, Y −1.5e−9..2.22 m, Z −1.06..1.314 m. Those are mesh bounds, not withers height, historical dimensions or fitted collision extents.

The driving harness is 602,980 bytes, 8,012 triangles and 36 separately drawn parts. Its head/root binding and ten horse sockets remain provisional. Reins, checks and traces have unfinished dynamic binding; full source-plate construction, pressure/load behavior, riding tack, collars/hames, LOD derivatives and a canonical authored master remain open. The current sockets are engineering targets, not fit acceptance.

## Dated source and image decisions

The register includes exact local-file hashes, public locators, inspected rendition sizes, creation/depicted dates, rights, permitted use and omissions. The five requested historical leads were viewed again for this handoff: Chrimes carriages, Smith ice team, Harvey-frontage carriage, disputed stable lineup and Sauganash drawing.

Chrimes: two distinct head profiles are visible in the left turnout, while the right turnout visibly has one horse. This supports the current reader's left-pair interpretation, but overlapping shafts/traces and the old disagreement remain recorded; it does not upgrade ambiguous coupling to an attested mechanical arrangement. The after-1872 broad date and unknown/link-only rights remain. Smith visibly has two horses and the business lettering; breed, owner and exact tack dimensions do not follow. Harvey shows a side-on single horse, shafts/reins and occupied raised front seat; the frontage is not vehicle ownership. The stable lineup shows five standing horses, handlers and a two-wheel cart; disputed Glessner attribution is not reinstated. Sauganash is an undated retrospective drawing with a partial saddled horse at the post, not an 1835 photograph or a complete saddle pattern.

The earlier viewer survey screened 24/24 pre-fire records and 83 selected Prairie images after searching 944 metadata records. Its ledger remains the coverage record, not a claim that every Prairie image or every map was examined at archive resolution. No contemporary pre-fire horse modeling photograph was found in that screening; this is an evidence gap, not absence of horses.

New direct public Internet Archive requests now return 200 for Moore metadata, OCR XML and selected page JPEGs. Printed p.1 confirms No.1's folded/stitched breastcollar, breeching/girth, straight stitched 1-inch traces, flat reins and round check rein. Printed p.16 No.100 explicitly describes patent-leather collars, plated nickel hames and 1¼-inch traces for English Style Coach Harness. Printed p.34 supplies an actual English riding-saddle engraving and price, not its underside/tree or exact dimensions. The requested image index `n` follows the zero-based DjVu object sequence; `n3.jpg` is printed p.2 even though scandata leaf 3 was initially assumed to be p.1. Unknown-rights local photographs remain text/cross-check leads; no pixels, textures, tracing or meshes are derived from them. The cleared modern anatomy and public-domain plates allow original modeling now. Later books and modern photographs cannot certify an early Chicago phenotype or gear variant.

## Executable modeling specification

The adjacent `horse-master-spec.json` contains the frozen frame, dimensional targets, exact baseline interface snapshot, proposed production hierarchy, material/LOD ceilings, source choices, outputs and acceptance sequence. It is modeling input, not a new live app schema or an assertion that a production exporter exists.

Author in metres, right-handed +Y up, +Z forward, anatomical left +X. Ground root is below the midpoint of hoof support centres. Bake unit node scales; no ENU offset is embedded in the master. Scene ENU east maps +X, north maps −Z; with a +Z-forward animal, a clockwise-from-north heading maps to actor yaw `π − heading`. Do not reuse a building's facade yaw. The origin is neither the saddle nor a vehicle axle.

First engineering targets: withers 1.55 m; shoulder-to-buttock horizontal length 1.60 m; trunk breadth 0.55 m, with declared 0.02/0.04/0.04 m authoring tolerances. They bound a reconstruction, not a historical measurement. Four actual neutral sole surfaces—not relocated proxy markers—must meet the fixture ground within 2 mm. Store actual body dimensions and all fitted transforms after authoring; do not nonuniformly stretch one rig to manufacture a draft breed.

Start with the continuous body silhouette: withers/scapular shoulder flow into breast and barrel; pelvis/croup volume; anatomically differentiated fore/hind limbs; closed muzzle, modeled nostrils and lips; eyelid margins surrounding inset eyes; pinna cavities and ear roots; heels, frog/sole and hoof wall transitions; separate mane/tail hair silhouette. Eliminate disconnected primitive collars, cylindrical joints, button eyes, open-mouth proxy gaps and slab-like mane before high-frequency coat detail. Keep visible source-supported shape and disclosed unseen-side reconstruction separate. Preserve topology ownership and avoid hiding crossings by arbitrary body deletion.

The proposed production rig adds pelvis/spine/withers/neck, jaw/ears/eyes, proper scapula/humerus/radius/carpus/cannon/fetlock/pastern/hoof fore chains, and femur/tibia/hock/cannon/fetlock/pastern/hoof hind chains. It is deliberately separate from the existing 15-joint fixture and humanoid rig. Final names/parentage are in JSON; preserve a documented old-to-new migration/retarget map rather than claiming the current clips fit automatically. Four normalized influences per vertex; all LODs retain the full contract rig and semantic sockets.

Materials: original UV/PBR coat, eyes, hooves and mane/tail; leather and metal belong to separate tack. Coat/hoof/leather are dielectric. Do not bake directional highlights into albedo, use chrome for all metal, derive colors from sepia, or assume a large texture makes the body photographic. Proposed body-only LOD ceilings are 60k/25k/8k/2k triangles, maximum six material draws at near LOD, with up-to-4K/2K/1K/512 texture edges. Measure compressed bytes and decoded memory separately, share maps where appropriate and revise ceilings only with a recorded measurement. Four full RGBA8 4K maps with mipmaps would already be about 341 MiB; the spec does not authorize that layout. Original prototype material flat colors are not final skin/fur.

## Socket and vehicle handoff

Each socket stores a named node, parent, local translation/quaternion and role. Horse sockets: bit left/right on head; saddle on back; rider seat and stirrup targets on the fitted saddle; traces, breeching and shaft-tug targets on fitted harness. Loaded socket transforms follow their parent bones. Root-following old targets are not automatically a solved animated fit. Reins connect bit endpoints to actual human grip targets; wrist heads are not palm grips. Tension, slack and load path require separate validation.

Current W01 straight team hitch is tongue-local `[0,0,2.75]`, after steering origin `[0,.535,1.175]` and tongue origin `[0,0,.35]`, yielding root `[0,.535,4.275]`. Its single-horse socket is reserved, not supported. The parked tongue is pitched +0.1857844468 rad around X, placing its empty endpoint near the ground: a standing team cannot be attached to that parked pose unchanged. Current C02 has a separate single-horse profile: steering root `[0,.45,.95]`, identity drawgear, hitch-local `[0,.3,2.2]`, yielding root `[0,.75,3.15]`. The named 1893 Studebaker Brougham plate shows a pair/pole; C02's single-shaft profile is not its exact configuration. Covered W01 variants explicitly disable occupied/hitch/boarding/collision interfaces even though inherited node names remain.

Place hoof roots on terrain first; solve supported pole/shaft/trace geometry and full local-to-world socket transforms with actual compatible fittings. Do not align unlike root positions, scale a horse to erase a height mismatch, or conflate a flexible trace end with a rigid root frame. Test swept bodies/legs/pole/shafts throughout turns and gait. Coupling capability stays false until those tests pass.

## Motion, driver and export acceptance

Production clips are in place. Required first idle/walk, then trot/start/stop/turn, use stable names with speed, cycle, stride, contact phases and measured reference scope. Existing prototype walk is 1.25 s/0.9290 m s⁻¹, trot 0.72 s/1.8162 m s⁻¹; they are synthetic engineering values, not sourced biological gait. They remain regression inputs. Require speed×cycle≈stride, expected footfall ordering, continuous planted-hoof contact, no joint hyperextension, and body/saddle/driver movement considered together. Neutral actual soles ≤2 mm from ground, planted-hoof drift ≤20 mm, loop-root translation ≤1 mm and yaw ≤0.1° are proposed gates, not new passing results.

Reuse existing reconstructed 1835 resident identities when a suitable driver/rider is needed. Preserve identity grade and date; do not claim an archival likeness, actual occupation or witnessed ride. The W01 named seated study already uses Bardwell, Dufresne and Flynn, with wrist—not finger-grip—targets; actual exported garment/shoe/seat fit is a separate gate. Scene examples in 1812/1835/1904 require their own dated wardrobe/tack and provenance. Glessner is not automatically a driver.

Preserve a canonical authoring source and original licensed texture sources, then produce self-contained GLB plus engine-neutral sidecar by a pinned command. A Blender master uses a qualified bake executor; the project's original authored-source/mesh exception is also available when its recipe, source inputs, licence and deterministic portable outputs are declared and reviewed. T-2800 does not require inventing a new Blender-only blocker. Do not invoke the humanoid-only exporter as if a horse passed its skeleton contract, and do not install Blender on this runner. The current NumPy/std-library prototype commands remain reproducible regression paths; selecting a production authoring route does not itself qualify their anatomy.

For each source/derivative LOD record bytes, triangles, draws, bones, dimensions, decoded texture memory, actual master hashes and closure. Validate complete buffers/skins/animations, outward surface geometry, real normals, licence/source IDs and rest/socket parity. Matched source/pose/camera comparisons must show whole body and close head/hoof/tack views at desktop 1280×800 and mobile 390×780. Proposed visible-landmark RMS≤1% image height/max≤2% and silhouette IoU≥.95 are supporting measurements; visible mismatches still block the photographic ticket. Store occlusion masks and never tune a camera to conceal a mismatch. Measure moving CPU/GPU/memory at full/balanced/light and near/mid/far distances; a translating frozen walk pose is not animation acceptance.

## Next implementation sequence and what remains open

1. T-2800: build and inspect the original continuous, anatomy-backed neutral horse first, with actual source/pose/camera pairs. Propose the model/mesh/source package for root review; do not repeat an indefinite material loop over the rejected primitive body.
2. T-2801/T-2802: fit separate 1891 light buggy harness, coach collar/hames and riding-saddle families as applicable. The first English saddle engraving bounds the visible upper silhouette; underside/tree, girth and stirrup-leather routing are declared reconstruction, with collision/contact proof. Earlier tack remains a separate researched/reconstructed variant.
3. T-2803: rig/retarget production anatomy and prove contact-supported idle/walk/trot before a long pose/gear matrix. Old sampled-fit successes and failures remain tied to their exact model hashes.
4. T-2804/T-2795: fit an existing period-clothed human, real rein endpoints and a compatible vehicle hitch; then validate running gear, turns and gait together.
5. T-2796/T-2797/T-2805: enable distant traffic and eventually boarding/mounting/ride/dismount only after those assets/interfaces qualify. Camera paces are not a substitute.

T-2799 can be reviewed as a bounded research and modeling-specification deliverable after its source records, inventory and packet are integrated and gated. T-2800–T-2805, photographic anatomy/materials, source-matched finished tack, full motion/LOD fit, continuous collision, physical pressure/load suitability, all-era town examples and occupied interaction remain open.
