More wagons, all over a frontier town.

**The owner, 2026-08-18, verbatim: "there can be more wagons! of course there would be
more wagons all over the place in a frontier town."** This widens T-0040 (done), which
placed wagons only at the twenty-six doors the ordinance attested. The restraint is
overridden; reconstructed is the tier; label and mark.

The brief shows the range (`data/sources/assets/owner_brief_2026_08_18/README.md`):
an **ox-drawn covered wagon train** on the river street (image 11), a **covered wagon
under the Green Tree's open shed** and farm wagons in its yard (image 7), a covered
wagon on the open road (image 12).

Spread wagons through the town: on streets, at yards, by stores — varied types (farm
box wagons, covered emigrant wagons, carts), varied orientations, some with ox-yokes or
shafts down. No draft animals attached unless fauna work already supports them; never
people (L1).

**Acceptance:** wagons appear across the town rather than only at the attested doors —
a walk down two core streets passes several, varied in type — each carded reconstructed.
Gates green.
