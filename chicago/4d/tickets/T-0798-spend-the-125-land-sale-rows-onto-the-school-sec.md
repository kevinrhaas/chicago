*"lot 1 of block 83 of the school section (sec 16 T39N R14E)"* — each marked *"Read, and not put
on the ground."* The October 1833 auction was read in full (191 rows landed 2026-09-04) and every
placement waited on a grid that did not exist. This is the owner's standing complaint about reading
with no output, with the output already named on each row.

## The ask

1. Resolve every row that names a block (and lot, where it does) to T-0797's polygon: the
   purchaser's name onto ground, dated to the sale, with the row cited. Where the row names a block
   the sheet numbers differently from the sale record — the tear at 87/88 is the likely place —
   write the disagreement and refuse, never pick.
2. A `--report` that says how many of the 125 landed, how many were refused and why, and which
   blocks changed hands most — the first honest statement of *who owned the south in 1835*.
3. Grade every placement as what it is: a purchaser's name on a lot in 1833 is not a resident, a
   house or a trade in 1835. Nothing here mints a person or a roof.

**Acceptance:** the 125 rows are each on a polygon or refused by name, the report exists, and no
row is placed by guessing a number the sheet does not carry.
