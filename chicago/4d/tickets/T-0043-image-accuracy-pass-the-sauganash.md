Image-accuracy pass: the Sauganash.

Piece 2 of 3 of **T-0006 — Image-accuracy passes on the landmark buildings**, split
because the parent needed more than one run's demonstration to be done. The parent keeps
the full ask and its links; this ticket owns one slice of it.

**Enriched by the owner's brief of 2026-08-18** (three Sauganash views —
`data/sources/assets/owner_brief_2026_08_18/README.md`, images 8, 9, 10: the Petford
1831 watercolour, the Braunhold/Andreas 1884 engraving, the Trowbridge drawing). The
owner, verbatim: **"also for the sauganash, the same, so many details of posts and porch
and fences and trees and landings, make sure saugansh has as much as you can from these
photos, add that to your focused sauganash runs."**

The building, where the views agree (agreement across three independent views is the
strongest inference this tier allows):

- the clapboard two-storey block **with the log wing attached at the left** — the wing
  is squared logs, its own chimney, its own door to grade, a shed-roofed porch hood over
  that door (image 9);
- a small flat-hooded **entrance frontispiece** on the main block's front door (images
  8, 9);
- **brick chimneys**; the Petford view gives the roof a dark green/moss shingle tone;
- **louvred shutters** on the sash (image 10 — Trowbridge only; weaker, say so on the
  card).

The curtilage, which the owner's ask makes equal in rank:

- **plank sidewalks on both frontages** and a **board crossing over the road** (images
  8, 9 — the "landings");
- **hitching posts / corner posts** at the road edge;
- a **vertical-board fence** running from the building line, enclosing the rear yard
  (all three views), **trees standing behind the fence**;
- the saddled horse at the post is L1 reference only.

Tier 5 rules as ever: form, materials, furniture — never a coordinate or footprint.
Cite via the brief README until T-0075 lands identifications.

**Acceptance:** a before/after pair from a stand matching the engraving's quarter — the
building corrected (log wing, frontispiece, chimneys, shutters where carded as weak) AND
its ground present (walks, crossing, posts, board fence, rear-yard trees) — every claim
at its honest tier. Gates green.
