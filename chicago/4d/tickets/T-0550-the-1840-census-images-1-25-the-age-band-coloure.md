**Acceptance:** `pages/33S7-9YYJ-9M5.json` (printed 229) and `pages/33S7-9YYJ-38.json` (printed 231)
carry, on every one of their 30 and 31 records, a reading of all 38 columns of the left sheet, cut at a
grid fitted to the sheet's own printing and checked against the printed heading before a cell is read;
`cells_column_check` states per column the read sum, the enumerator's own footing, how confidently that
footing was read, whether it balances and the residual; `cells` carries only the balanced columns and
`cells_first_pass` the whole reading; and the 30-versus-31 disagreement between the sheet and PR #670 on
printed 229 is answered from the column totals rather than from the ruling, with what they settle stated.
