Boats correct for the era, on the water.

**The owner, 2026-08-18, verbatim: "you can add boats correct for the era! they would
exist"** — and, of the drawbridge engravings, **"also note the boats there."** Liberal
reconstructed additions are the standing ruling; label and mark them as such.

The brief bounds the types (`data/sources/assets/owner_brief_2026_08_18/README.md`):

- **schooner-rigged sailing vessels** moored in the reach below the Dearborn drawbridge
  (images 2 and 3 — masts crowd that stretch of river);
- **rowboats/skiffs** on the water and drawn up at the bank (image 11, South Water 1834);
- **bark canoes** at the fort reach (the 2026-08-11 Fort Dearborn views already committed).

All moored, anchored or beached — **unmanned, L1 stands, no crews and no figures.** Hulls
and rigs at the reconstructed tier bounded by the engravings and by 1830s lake commerce
(schooners were the lake carriers; the first steamers were rare calls). Keep them off the
navigation span of the drawbridge so T-0071's bridge work stays clean.

**Acceptance:** era-correct boats sit on the water — at least a moored schooner near the
drawbridge reach and rowboats at a bank — each carding as reconstructed with its bounds;
zero pageerrors, gates green.
