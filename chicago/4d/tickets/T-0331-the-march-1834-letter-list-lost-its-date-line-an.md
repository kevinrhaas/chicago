T-0312 found a Chicago post-office letter list on page 4 of `chicago_democrat_1834_03_04`
that no earlier sweep had, because the segmenter cut its printed column down the middle and
took the heading with it — `List of L` at the head of column 2, `etters` / `ost-Offiee at
Chica-` / `34.` at the head of column 3. It is minted as claims **c024 and c025**, one per
crop, and **78 personal names** are extracted over John S. C. Hogan's signature.

Three things are wrong with it and only the page images can fix any of them.

**1. WHICH RETURN IT IS CANNOT BE READ.** The date line survives as `34.` and nothing else.
The name set is neither the 1 January 1834 list nor a subset of it — Atkins, Benton, Conger,
Carrier, Crisey, Delano, Haight, Johnson, Killigoss, Knapp, Leena, Miller, Meriams, Machel,
M'Carty, Marshall, Nats, Pestin, Poel, Pease, Phelps, Riche, Rouit, Schermerhorn, Stowell,
Taylor, Thompson, Thorn, Vanzandt, Vandine, Wilson, Willece, Warren, Wrickey and Webb are all
new here, and much of January's list is absent — so a **1 March 1834 quarterly return** is the
obvious candidate and is not asserted. If it is one, this is a second census-proxy cohort for
the year before the scene date and T-0299's minting rule applies to it in its own right.

**2. BOTH CROPS FAILED VISION.** Each carries the transcription's own
`[uncertain: This column uses the Tesseract fallback …]` banner, and the fallback has
interleaved the list with whatever advertisement shared the printed column — the boot-and-shoe
notice, the Two Buildings to Let notice, G. Spring's office, the Athenian prospectus.

**3. EVERY NAME IS CUT ON ITS LEFT EDGE**, which is the opposite damage to
[[T-0318]]'s January list and worse: it is the FORENAME that is gone. Thirty-nine of the 78
are minted as `[?] Surname`, and eleven more as fragments — `[…]as Bennett`, `[…]ell Baldwin`,
`[…]nel Devoe`, `[…]l. Frazer`, `[…]s Miller`, `[…]y Meriams`, `[…]s Machel`, `[…]in Poel`,
`[…]ward`. None of them can be matched against another record, and none was completed from
January's list, because a completion that looks right proves nothing.

Orphan debris stands where names stood — `rackepelagd` at line 3218, `rhe ae` at 3225,
`1e) Ws` at 3276 — so **78 is a floor**, and a low one against January's 97.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — one demonstration, never weakened to pass)

- The list is read off the page images of PDF page 20 (issue page 4) of the 1834-03-04
  Democrat, and its **date line is read or reported unreadable at the image too**.
- The extracted count is stated against a hand count of the printed list.
- The 39 bare surnames and 11 cut fragments are either completed or reported unreadable.
- Claims c024 and c025 take `reading: scan_verified`, and `coverage.json`'s T-0312 note
  states a count and a return date instead of a floor and a candidate.
- If it proves to be a distinct return from 1 January's, that is said in the note, because a
  second cohort is worth more to the people work than a second printing of the first.
