The sibling of T-1198 for everything the reconstruction bands invented: the trade
households (T-1173), the families and widows (T-1171, T-1174), the
firms (T-1184 … T-1188), the boarding houses and hands (T-1187,
T-1175), the camps (T-1178). Every one of them stands at address-book rung 5 by
construction, and this ticket is where the policy decides WHICH lot — with the town's existing
roofs, the standing documented records and the known seats already on the grid.

**Rules:** `tools/seat_reconstructed_1835.py --build|--check` walks the order book's structure
buckets by division and street class in policy order: (1) the commercial front first — stores,
forwarding houses, taverns to the corners and the river faces of South Water, Lake, Dearborn,
North Water and the Canal approach, **packing the main-street lots to the density standard**
(three party-line units where the policy's multi-building rule fires, one where it does not),
adopting standing anonymous roofs of the right family before raising a slot for a new one;
(2) the professional and merchant houses to the H-family bands; (3) the tradesmen's cottages
(D3–D5) on the side streets near their shops; (4) labourers' cabins and shanties (D1/D2) on the
small lots of the additions and the fringes the policy names; (5) boarding houses near the
landings and approaches; (6) the noxious trades to the branches; (7) camps to the camp grounds
(T-1214); (8) outbuildings per household type behind the principal roof off the alley. Every
seat: lot id, `stands_on`, setback class, adopted structure id or `slot` (a slot is a request the
build tickets fulfil), seed, the policy rule id, and the order-book bucket decremented.

**Acceptance:**

- Every reconstructed household and business has an address-book row with a lot (or a camp
  ground); counts per policy rule and per district printed; the multi-building-lot rate on the
  principal streets printed against the density standard.
- The extended block recipes (`1835_platted_block_parcels.json`, the north/west parcels, new
  addition parcels) are REGENERATED by the tool from the seats — slots by family per lot — so the
  infill generators build exactly what the seating asked; `generate_*_infill.py --check` green
  (they refuse nothing: corridor, separation, no-build and buildable-ground tests pass on every
  seat, or the seat moves and the row says so).
- Deterministic; `--check` in `check.sh`; LIBERTIES entry with scope.
- **Visible:** the People and Businesses views' "Go to" resolves for every reconstructed card
  (to the lot, until the roof stands); the "Reconstructing the town" card shows seats vs roofs.

**Stop condition:** every person and firm in the town has a lot, and the build tickets hold a
complete, generator-ready slot list per block.
