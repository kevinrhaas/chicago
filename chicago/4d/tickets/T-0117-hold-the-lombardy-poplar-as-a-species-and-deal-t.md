Hold the Lombardy poplar as a species, and deal the planted rows the plates attest.

**Acceptance, stated 2026-08-24 before the work, and not weakened since.**

1. `Populus nigra` 'Italica' is HELD as a species with its own provenance: a record in a
   committed flora zone with a grade, resolving sources, a July phenology and a height
   band whose derivation is written down — and it reaches the reader that places it, so
   `measure_flora_reach.py` and `measure_planting_reach.py` both count it as reached
   rather than banked unread.
2. WHAT THE PLATES ATTEST IS ESTABLISHED BY MEASUREMENT, not from memory: every
   committed image that draws a poplar is opened and measured, and the count, the
   spacing and where the row stands relative to the house are reported as numbers. **If
   the plates will not carry the claim, the ticket says so plainly with the numbers** —
   a refutation is an acceptable outcome and may not be papered over.
3. The rows are DEALT BY A RULE, not listed: a generator holds the clauses, prints why
   each house got a row or did not, and `tools/check.sh` re-derives the record byte for
   byte. Every refusal is named with its reason.
4. A visitor walking the town sees the rows — tall, narrow, unmistakably not the round
   crowns around them — and every stem is graded and claimed in `docs/LIBERTIES.md`.
5. The triangle cost is MEASURED against the committed budget, not estimated, and no
   ceiling is breached silently.
6. `tools/check.sh` → CHECK PASS and the smoke's flora leg green, with no assertion,
   gate or baseline weakened to get there.

**Answered:** the treatment is attested in WORDS, which nobody had noticed — Wau-Bun,
ch. XVII: *"A broad green space was inclosed between it and the river, and shaded by a
row of Lombardy poplars."* The plates give the count (four) and the rhythm (a gap of
0.195 of the row's own height, sd 0.010 over five independently drawn views). **The
plates REFUSE a second location** — all seven draw the row at the one house, and that
house is excluded from this scene — so the addresses are the rule's, at the
`reconstructed` tier, and L181 says so.
