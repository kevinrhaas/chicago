The Dearborn drawbridge's own form, to its engravings: A-frames, chains, leaves, rails.

Piece 1 of 2 of **T-0071 — The Dearborn drawbridge area, improved to its engravings**, split because the parent needed more than one run's demonstration to be done. The parent keeps the full ask and its links; this ticket owns one slice of it.

The engravings the owner supplied (`data/sources/assets/owner_brief_2026_08_18/README.md`,
images 2 and 3) draw the 1834 crossing as **twin timber A-frames standing above the deck,
chains down to the lifting leaves**, with the deck railed. The mesh built on 2026-08-11
has the frames — two plain posts under a lapped head — and deliberately nothing else: no
bracing, no tackle, no leaf, no rail. Each of those omissions was argued from the silence
of the two TEXTS (Andreas and the chicagology transcription), which is the right reading
of a text and not the only evidence this project now holds.

Scope is the crossing itself: the frames' A-form, the hoist chains, the leaf division of
the closed draw, and the deck rail. The bank structures the same plates show are T-0133's.
The boats are T-0063's. The deck stays walkable end to end.

**Acceptance:** standing on either approach, the Dearborn bridge reads as the engravings'
gallows-frame drawbridge — braced A-frames, chains falling from each head, a draw that
reads as two leaves, and a railed deck — and it is still walkable end to end. Every new
attribute cards at its honest tier, the plate is named as the reasoning rather than cited
as a source record it does not have, and each invention has a `docs/LIBERTIES.md` entry.
Gates green.
