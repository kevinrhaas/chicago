hydrology.geojson's two confidence strings no longer match the generator that says it is generated.

Found by T-0684. `python3 tools/trace_river.py --check` reproduces
`river.geojson` byte for byte and reports `DIFF` on
`data/terrain/epochs/e1834_harbor_cut/hydrology.geojson`. The geometry is
identical to the millimetre. What differs is two strings on `north_side_slough`:

    committed                    generator writes
    width_confidence: inferred   width_confidence: reconstructed
    depth_confidence: reconstructed   depth_confidence: conjectural

The file's own `_doc` says "Generated by tools/trace_river.py — do not
hand-edit", so one of the two has drifted from the other and nothing gates it.
It matters because these are PROVENANCE grades, which this project treats as the
product: `depth_confidence` committed as `reconstructed` sits above the
generator's `conjectural`, and the generator's own note in the same file says
"Depth is conjectural: no source gives one" — the committed grade contradicts
the sentence beside it.

**Acceptance:**

1. Which of the two is right is decided on the evidence, not by taking whichever
   is newer — the width is read off the wash's interior distance transform (is
   that `inferred`, with the reasoning stated, or `reconstructed`?), and the
   depth is assumed with no source at all.
2. Whichever way it goes, `trace_river.py --check` comes back clean on both
   files, so the generator and the committed file agree again.
3. The gate learns to catch it. A file that declares itself generated and then
   silently stops matching its generator is exactly what `check.sh` should
   refuse; say why it currently cannot, and either wire it in or record the cost
   of doing so.
