Every age in the town answers to the person's own evidence: a roll, a trade or a press appearance is read before the kinship word a household happens to use, and an obituary's birth interval the card refutes is refused rather than spent.

Piece 1 of 3 of **T-1179 — Converge the reconstructed resident layer: index, sidecars, town census, People view and gates agree; every reconstructed person carries basis, seed, liberty and substitution rule; the population profile is re-run and the town reads complete against the model**, split because the parent needed more than one run's demonstration to be done. The parent keeps the full ask and its links; this ticket owns one slice of it.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — one demonstration, never weakened to pass)

**Acceptance:** (stated before working — one demonstration, never weakened to pass)

1. A pass that spends an interval onto a person reads the rest of the card first. Where
   an obituary matched to a name puts the person under the 15 that
   `data/reconstruction/1835_sex_age_model.json` requires of any roll naming somebody in
   their own right, the SPEND is refused: the `birth_year` block asserts no year, carries
   `refused_because` and `refused_interval`, and the crosswalk's match is untouched.
2. The franchise's 20 is NOT a refusal. The model holds that floor under the liberty
   `L-rc-age-conditioning` and says in capitals that no source states it, so an interval
   that clears 15 and misses 20 is CARRIED with `contradicted_by_the_card` beside it —
   T-1146's disposition, and the one the letter-list ruling settled on.
3. A man's own age about himself is never refused, only flagged.
4. The age DRAW reads evidence before kinship: `dependent_child_under_15` is chosen for a
   son, a daughter or a child only where the card names them nowhere in their own right.
5. Every card the two rules move re-derives, and the layer reaches a fixed point — the
   stage checks, `rebuild_resident_index`, the model, the profile, the order book, the
   transient cohort, the attribute tiers, and `rederive.mjs --check` after all of them.
6. Visible: the person's card prints the refusal in the Born row, in its own words, and
   the people the rules move carry the band their own evidence licenses.
7. Both rules are held by self-tests that fire when broken.

**Stop condition:** no person in this town holds a trade, stands on a poll list or sits in
the press at an age the same card refutes, and the disagreements that survive are printed
rather than resolved.

**What it found.** Eight people, and two of them are among the best-known in the town.
George Smith advertised the Exchange Coffee House in the Democrat of 5 November 1834 and
20 May 1835, and his card gave his birth as 1831: a coffee-house keeper of four. John S.
Wright holds an attested role of clerk and stands in the press over the scene date, and
was drawn out of the 1840 schedule's `free white males 10 under 15` column because his
father's card calls him a son. Amasa Wright paid tax in 1833 aged five and Caleb Harrison
had letters waiting for him at ten. Erastus Bowen, Isaac Dewey Harmon, Edward A. Rogers,
Jeremiah Smith and Alexander Wolcott each miss the franchise's line by a year or five, and
each is carried with the disagreement printed instead.
