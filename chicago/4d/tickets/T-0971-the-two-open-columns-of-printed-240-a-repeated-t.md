The two open columns of printed 240: a repeated two-stroke figure on four cells that closes m_20_30 at 41 or m_30_40 at 13, never both.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — the definition of done, never weakened to pass)

Printed 240 (`33SQ-GYYJ-CK`, T-0965) closes sixteen of its eighteen footed columns exactly. Two do
not, and they fail on one cause: a repeated figure — a long diagonal with a second, shorter stroke
above and parallel to it, or joined to it as a hook — standing in four cells:

| line | column | household as read |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | `m_30_40` | James [?]otley |
| 24 | `m_20_30` | T. M. Monta[?]on |
| 25 | `m_20_30` | T. M. Ma[?]kh[?]m |
| 26 | `m_20_30` | J. H. [?]ain |

All four are recorded `null` — unread, not blank, not guessed.

**The fork, exactly.** If the form is **11**, `m_20_30` closes at **41** with its three instances at 11
each (8 settled + 33), and the footing's first figure is this leaf's 4 — two joined diagonals closed by
a foot, the form the female footing row prints plainly at `f_50_60`. If the form is **1**, `m_30_40`
closes at **13**, which is what its own footing plainly prints (a bare diagonal beside this leaf's 3,
two stacked bowls, the same mark line 16 carries in `m_u5`). **No single value satisfies both**, and
31 — the other reading of the `m_20_30` footing — closes on no value at all, since 23 is not divisible
by three.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — one demonstration, never weakened to pass)
either the four cells carry values and both columns close on their printed footings, or the leaf is
shown not to settle them and the record says which of the two footings is wrong. A reading that closes
one column by leaving the other open is what already exists and is not the demonstration.

**Where to start.** The female footing row is the clearest writing on the leaf and fixes the alphabet:
8, 6, 4, 1, 9, 6, 2 across `f_u5`..`f_40_50`, then 4 and 1. `33SQ-GYYJ-BP` (T-0964) separates a
JOINED pair of diagonals (4) from two SEPARATE ones (11) in the same footing row; whether this leaf's
hand makes that same distinction is the question. The leaf's own `column_reconciliation` and
`reconciliation` carry the measurements.
