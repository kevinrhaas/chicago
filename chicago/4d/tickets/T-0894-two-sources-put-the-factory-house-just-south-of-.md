Two sources put the Factory House just SOUTH of Fort Dearborn and jb_beaubien_homestead stands north of it: that bearing is all that is left of the identity question, and form.stories waits on it.

**Opened by T-0718, 2026-09-06**, as the residue of the identity question it answered.

T-0718 disposed of the John Dean premise: the "John-Dean house" is a third building — a five-room
house an army contractor named Dean built at the lake shore near the foot of Randolph Street in
1815 (Andreas scan p. 183) — so Wentworth's *"after he moved from what was before known as the
John-Dean house"* does not say Beaubien left the Factor House. Read forward, Andreas p. 205 and
`bk_afc_009` both have the factory building reach Beaubien in 1822 and become his dwelling, and
Wentworth's "traditional residence" is then that building, at the north-east corner this record
now stands on.

**What still refuses to close is a bearing.** Andreas scan p. 191 — *"the building, which was just
south of the fort"* — and Hurlbut's Hubbard, `bk_afc_009` — *"the Factory House, located just south
of Fort Dearborn"* — both put it SOUTH of the fort. `jb_beaubien_homestead` stands about 23 m NORTH
of the committed palisade. Two independent passages agree the 1839 homestead was at South Water and
Michigan; two agree the Factory House was south of the fort; and nothing reached reconciles them.

**Two things wait on the answer.** `form.stories` is `1` against `bk_hub_063`'s *"two-story,
squared-log structure"*, which describes the Factor House and would describe this record if the
identity settled that way — and a storey change is a bake. And `aka` still calls this "the American
Fur Company factory building".

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — one demonstration, never weakened to pass)

- A stated finding on the bearing, with its confidence and its reasoning, or "not established" in
  as many words — not a preference for whichever source agrees with the committed position.
- `form.stories` settled either way, and if it moves, the bake and the publish move with it.
- Nothing regraded to make the answer look tidier; the corner stays `inferred` whatever happens.
