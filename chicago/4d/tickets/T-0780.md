dev's gate is red: PR #882 merged a duplicate west_water street entry and left four derived files unregenerated behind the moved waterline.

**What is red, and since when.** `tools/check.sh` on clean `origin/dev` at `f98d0c650` exits 1 on
four steps that no branch touched:

- `the yard goods re-derive from the rule that chose their frontages`
- `the frontage works re-derive from the rule that chose their walls`
- `north water street is still the line its own derivation produces, and still dry`
- `sidecars derived from data/` — reporting `data/streets/1835.json: missing or duplicate street id 'west_water'`

**Two causes, both from #882 (T-0686, `f046ef78e`), and the second is the interesting one.**

1. **A duplicate street record.** #882's only change to `data/streets/1835.json` is `+15 −0`: the
   whole `west_water` object, byte-identical to the one #875 (T-0445) had already committed, pasted
   in immediately after it. That is a merge resolution taking both sides of an addition. `west_water`
   is the twelfth AND the thirteenth entry in `streets[]`, and `compile_scene.py` refuses a duplicate
   id, which is what took the sidecars step down and with it every card the sidecars feed.

2. **Four derived files left behind a waterline that moved.** #882's real work was a repair to the
   Wright 1834 bank trace — the 205 px seam strip on the South Branch's east bank and a 180 px
   fragment on the north bank of the main stem, both put back. Its own note lists the derivations it
   re-ran and records `frontage works: no change`. That was measured on the branch and is not what
   the merged tree says: `north_water`'s centreline is derived by offsetting the committed north
   bank half a platted module, so a bank that moves moves the street, and the street's edge, walks,
   crossings and fences move with it — and the dooryard plantings and yard goods are dealt off those
   frontages in turn.

  rewritten by its own generator in the same commit.
- The removal restores `data/streets/1835.json` byte-for-byte to its pre-#882 content, and the diff
