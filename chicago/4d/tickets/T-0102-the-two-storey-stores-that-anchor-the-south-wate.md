The two-storey stores that anchor the South Water row's east end.

Piece 2 of 2 of **T-0078 — The South Water river row, from the 1834 view**. T-0101 landed the
row; this is the half of the view it could not build.

The reference puts **two two-storey frame stores anchoring the east end** of the row. The five
South Water blocks cannot produce them: their schedule mix, drawn from the 665-roof programme, is
D-family dwellings throughout — no C3 (narrow two-storey store), no C4, no D7 — and
`check_block` holds a parcel to the mix it claims. So this needs one of two things, and choosing
between them is the work:

1. **A schedule change** — reconcile the 665-roof programme so a store family is dealt to the
   east end of this row, and argue why the town's business front takes it from wherever it was.
2. **A documented liberty growing the total**, under the owner's standing ruling of 2026-08-18
   that reconstructed items may be added liberally when he asks, with the growth recorded.

**Acceptance:** two two-storey frame stores stand at the east end of the South Water row, on its
line and in its rhythm, either dealt by the schedule or added under a liberty that says so in
`docs/LIBERTIES.md`. Gates green.
