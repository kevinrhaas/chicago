S. W. Sherman never wrote a nought at the foot of 6H or 5V, so the only footing nought in the deposit is on a leaf whose hand is unestablished: settle whether 33S7-9YYJ-8D is his.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — the definition of done, never weakened to pass)

- Whether `33S7-9YYJ-8D` is in S. W. Sherman's hand is decided on EVIDENCE, not on adjacency in the
  film: its heading names no enumerator, so the argument has to be made from the writing — the forms
  of the digits arithmetic already labels on both leaves, the printed-form fill pattern, and the rules
  and blanks the same clerk leaves the same way.
- The answer is committed with its confidence and its reasoning on `33S7-9YYJ-8D.json`, and it is
  committed EITHER WAY. "Probably the same clerk" is not an answer; "unestablished, and here is what
  was tried" is.
- If the hands are the same, 8D's TOTAL footing 106 becomes an admissible comparator for this hand's
  footing nought, and T-0754's record is updated to say so. If they are not, T-0754's record is
  updated to drop it.

**Why:** T-0754 needed to know what S. W. Sherman's nought looks like at the FOOT of a leaf, where his
pen is drier and larger than in the body. He wrote none on 6H or 5V, the two leaves whose printed
headings name him. The only footing nought in the deposit is on 8D, whose heading names nobody — and
T-0652 already exists because a 6H finding was refused generalisation to 8D without a reading of 8D.
So the comparator is either good evidence or none, and which it is has never been settled.
