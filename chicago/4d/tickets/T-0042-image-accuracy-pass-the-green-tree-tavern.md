Image-accuracy pass: the Green Tree Tavern.

Piece 1 of 3 of **T-0006 — Image-accuracy passes on the landmark buildings**, split
because the parent needed more than one run's demonstration to be done. The parent keeps
the full ask and its links; this ticket owns one slice of it.

**Enriched by the owner's brief of 2026-08-18** (two new Green Tree views —
`data/sources/assets/owner_brief_2026_08_18/README.md`, images 6 and 7 — alongside
plate "11" already committed in the 2026-08-11 set). The owner, verbatim: **"for your
building accuracy sweeps, take a look at the green tree image. note not just the
buildings but the surrounding landscape, trees, porch deck sidewalks and make sure
those areas are rendered correctly."** The sweep's bar is the building AND its ground.

The building (all three views agree): two full storeys of pale clapboard, even 6-over-6
bays, end chimneys at both gables; a lower gabled wing at the left with a wide carriage
door.

The curtilage, which this pass must also close:

- the **post-mounted hanging signboard** at the street corner (name on the board — the
  T-0066 standard applies here first);
- **plank sidewalks with board crossings** on the frontages (image 6);
- an **open-sided wagon shed attached at the left, a covered wagon under it**, farm
  wagons in the yard (image 7);
- a **bench against the front wall** (the sitters are L1 reference only — the bench is
  the buildable fact);
- **dirt road with grass verges**, brush and small trees at the fence lines;
- image 6 shows **ship masts directly behind the roofline** — the river frontage is that
  close; check what the model puts behind the inn.

Tier 5 rules: the views drive massing, fenestration, materials and setting as
`inferred`/reconstructed, never a coordinate or footprint. Cite via the brief README
until T-0075 lands the plate identifications.

**Acceptance:** a before/after pair from a stand matching the engraving's quarter — the
building corrected to the views AND its surroundings (sign, walks, shed, yard, verges)
present — with every claim carded at its honest tier. Gates green.
