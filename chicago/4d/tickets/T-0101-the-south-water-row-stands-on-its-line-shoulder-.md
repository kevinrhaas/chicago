parent needed more than one run's demonstration to be done: the row itself and its two-storey
east anchor are separate claims with separate costs, and only the first can be built from what
the five blocks were dealt. The parent keeps the full ask and its links.

one-storey log and frame buildings shoulder to shoulder facing the river. The render had the
same reach as detached cottages 6-8 m behind the frontage, 14-20 m apart, each facing the
interior of its own block.

blocks between Franklin and State stand ON the frontage — one line, party walls, the face's own
bearing so the fronts look at the river — carded reconstructed throughout, with the line, the
bearing and the anchors read from the committed block boundary rather than authored. Gates green.
