Six South Water placements come off the modern kerb and onto the committed plat.

Piece 1 of 2 of **T-0127 — The rest of the town gets the street edge**, split because the parent needed more than one run's demonstration to be done. The parent keeps the full ask and its links; this ticket owns one slice of it.

**Acceptance:** the South Water Street placements that CAN be reconciled with the committed plat
are — each by re-deriving the coordinate its own record already describes against this project's
committed street centreline instead of the modern OpenStreetMap one, with no confidence grade and
no along-street position moved — and each that cannot is refused IN WRITING, per store, in its own
`position.note` and in `data/frontage/town_street_edge.json` `refused`, with the metres it would
move and the clause that refused it; the plank sidewalk closes on at least one whole platted block
face of South Water as a result, asserted by standing the walker on it end to end rather than by
counting records; the shadow lever T-0115 costed for T-0127 is implemented as a per-mesh opt-out
and MEASURED in that ticket's ledger, with the smoke's furniture-shadow check counting the exempt
meshes rather than assuming every furniture mesh casts; and all three detail tiers stay inside
their ceilings. Never by weakening a gate.

**What this piece deliberately does NOT carry.** The second street tier — T-0127's clause 2 — was
built, published and measured on this branch and does NOT fit: Randolph Street takes `full` to
1,497,588 of 1,400,000 and `balanced` to 1,355,638 of 1,210,000 at the axial stand, and the lever
above is worth nowhere near the shortfall. That reading, and the finding behind it (`balanced`
stood 4,238 triangles inside its ceiling BEFORE any of this), belong to their own ticket. South
Water's walk running unbroken end to end is T-0189.
