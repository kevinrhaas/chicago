## The ask

Off the registered full sheet (T-0787), with `tools/trace_shoreline.py`'s own method and spliced
byte-for-byte onto the existing trace at the forks box, one continuous run: **both pier lines**,
**the cut** between them, **the sand bar's outline to its southern tip**, **the old channel's west
bank to where Wright closes it** near Madison's line, and **the lake shore from there to the sheet's
bottom margin** — the brown Fractional Section 15 strip's east edge. No window boundary anywhere in
it. Where the old channel ends is written as the sheet draws it, dated 1834, graded for what a
year's silting could do, with the dossier's *"silting into a lagoon/slough"* beside it.

**Acceptance:** `shoreline.geojson`'s harbour polygon and both shore runs no longer carry a
window edge; the old channel closes; the south shore run reaches the sheet's margin; every vertex
west of E +314 is unchanged. Terrain re-bake belongs to T-0800.
