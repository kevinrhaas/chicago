Georeference the Fort Dearborn evacuation route south along the 1812 lakeshore and represent the battle site as an evidence-weighted corridor/zone rather than one falsely exact pin. Reconcile the traditional 18th/Prairie-Calumet marker area with historical accounts, maps and later scholarship.

The output is documentary geography: route polyline(s), uncertainty envelope, dated interpretive records and source notes. It may be visible as a route/interpretive overlay or card, but it must not imply that every reported phase of the fighting can be located to modern-lot precision.

Acceptance: a visitor can follow a sourced route from the first fort to the approximate battle area; the confidence/uncertainty is visible in the evidence model; exact and approximate claims are distinguished; and the route uses the reconstructed 1812 shoreline rather than modern streets as its primary geography.
