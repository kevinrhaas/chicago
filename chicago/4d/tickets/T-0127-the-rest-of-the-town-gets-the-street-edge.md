The rest of the town gets the street edge.

The remainder of **T-0069** (*"Fences line the streets, and plank sidewalks run beside
them"*), which laid the street edge along **South Water Street and Lake Street, Market to
State** — 1,147.7 m of plank sidewalk at the lot line, 9 board crossings, 12 street-lining
board fences — as `data/frontage/town_street_edge.json`, generated by
`tools/generate_frontage_works.py` from the committed plat grid and re-derived byte for
byte on every commit.

The owner's ask, verbatim, of the first Cook County jail engraving: *"note the fences
lining the street and what appears to be plank sidewalks. all of the streets should be
updated like this... at least south of the river or near the river."* **"All of the
streets"** is what this ticket is for. The rule is written, generated and gated; what is
left is more faces and one piece of real repair.

**What is left, and why each piece was left.**

1. **THE SOUTH WATER PLACEMENTS vs THE PLAT — the repair, and the only piece here that is
   not just more faces.** The walk on South Water's frontages comes out in pieces (46–71 m
   of a 97 m face) because ten documented buildings on that side were placed against the
   MODERN kerb, read off OpenStreetMap, rather than against this project's own platted
   line, and stand up to **6.9 m out past it** — nearly to the travelled track:
   `carpenter_south_water_store`, `h_jones_store`, `peck_store`,
   `jh_kinzie_forwarding_store`, `harmon_loomis_store`, `pruyne_kimball_drugstore`,
   `chicago_american_office`, `chicago_democrat_office`, `frederick_thomas_shop`,
   `madore_beaubien_house`. The march refuses those steps in writing and names the
   building in each one, so the disagreement is visible rather than hidden. Reconciling
   them with the committed plat closes those gaps and is worth doing on its own terms —
   the disagreement is a finding about the dataset, not about the sidewalk.
2. **RANDOLPH STREET and WASHINGTON STREET** — eight more platted block faces of the South
   Division, the same rule and no new argument.
3. **THE CROSS STREETS' OWN FRONTAGES** — Market, Franklin, Wells, La Salle, Clark,
   Dearborn and State each have two faces per block that this record does not touch, so a
   walker turning a corner steps off the boards. This is the largest piece by metres.
4. **THE WEST DIVISION** — `blk_lake_clinton`, across the South Branch, refused in T-0069
   as one stranded block rather than drawn alone.
5. **HITCHING POSTS AT THE COMMERCIAL FRONTS** — T-0069 allowed them to ride along and
   they were left out to keep that parcel to one demonstration. The Sauganash's two are
   already drawn from its own plates (L136); the rule would put them in the verge outside
   the walk at the trading frontages.

**What it will cost, and the lever that is already costed for it.** T-0069 measured 78
draw calls at the release gate's stand against the ceiling the owner raised the same day
from 80 to 120, with `light` at 576,335 of 600,000. More faces spend both. The lever
T-0115's ledger names is worth taking first: a plank walk lies 11 cm proud of the ground
and its own cast shadow is about 4 cm wide at noon, so if the ground-hugging furniture
stopped casting into the shadow map while the standing furniture (the fences) kept
casting, this layer's six shadow-pass calls at that stand and roughly half its triangles
at `full`/`balanced` would go for nothing a visitor can see. It is not free to implement:
`applyShadowTier` in `main.js` overwrites `castShadow` on every furniture mesh at every
tier, so it needs a per-mesh opt-out, and the smoke's *"the light tier draws no furniture
into the shadow map"* check needs to count the exempt meshes explicitly rather than assume
every furniture mesh casts.

**Acceptance:** the South Water placements are reconciled with the committed plat (or each
refused in writing, per store) and that street's walk runs unbroken except where its own
ground refuses it; at least one more street tier (Randolph, or the cross streets) carries
the street edge too; the walk is walkable end to end on each and board crossings carry the
walker round the corners; and all three detail tiers stay inside their ceilings with the
draw-call count inside whatever the budget then is — with the lever that paid for it named
and measured in T-0115's ledger. Never by weakening a gate.

## 2026-08-24 — SPLIT into five, because the acceptance names more than one demonstration

The acceptance above asks for the South Water placement repair AND at least one more street
tier AND the walkability of both AND the budget lever measured. That is not one run: the
repair rewrites ten documented records' positions and re-derives every gate that reads them,
while "one more street tier" is a generator boundary and its own metres. The ticket's own
body already enumerates the five pieces; the split takes them verbatim and changes no
priority — the children hold this ticket's exact place in QUEUE.md.

- **T-0190** the South Water placements vs the plat (the repair — body item 1)
- **T-0191** Randolph and Washington (item 2)
- **T-0192** the cross streets' own frontages (item 3, and the piece that must spend the
  T-0115 shadow lever, since it is the largest by metres)
- **T-0193** `blk_lake_clinton`, the West Division block (item 4)
- **T-0194** hitching posts at the commercial fronts (item 5)

This record keeps the owner's ask, the reference images and the links; the children own the
work.
