**Owner, 2026-09-17:** "my original concept is that the research is done so this is a check
and prep before we get into reconstruction so we can build the city and we are caught here …
make them good but not great."

This folds **eight** open tickets into one and sets a stop condition, because the queue had
turned one job into a leaf-by-leaf programme: T-0761, T-0984, T-0957, T-0942, T-0926, T-0934,
T-0944, T-0971. Every one is a single page or column of the **1840** federal census — a
footing that reads 198 against a column of 193, a No. of Scholars figure lost to the gutter,
a two-stroke figure on four cells that closes `m_20_30` at 41 or at 44.

**The proportion is the argument.** The scene is 1 July **1835**. The 1840 census is five
years after it, and nothing in it seats a person, dates an arrival or names a trade in 1835 —
it BRACKETS a household that other sources already put in the town. A column that closes at
41 rather than 44 moves no card. Eight tickets of single-leaf adjudication buy a precision
the reconstruction cannot spend.

**Each folded ticket's evidence is kept.** Its file stays in the repository with its findings
intact, marked `withdrawn` and naming this ticket, so a later run that genuinely needs one
page can read what was already established and pick it up again. Nothing is deleted and no
finding is overturned.

**Acceptance — one run, and it ends in a written gap, not a clean sheet:**

1. Read what reads cleanly on ONE pass at the standard already set. No second exposure, no
   re-imaging, no per-leaf ticket.
2. Everything that does not close — a footing that disagrees, a figure lost to a gutter, a
   column with two defensible readings — is written into ONE residue table with the page, the
   competing readings and why it was not settled. **That table is the deliverable.**
3. State the residue's effect on the 1835 town in one sentence per class. If the answer is
   "none", say so, and that is a complete answer.
4. **No new ticket may be filed for any residue row.** A row that later blocks real
   reconstruction work is picked up by the ticket that is blocked, not by a refill here.

**Stop condition:** the residue table exists and the 1840 census is closed for the purposes of
the 1835 reconstruction. Good, not great, on purpose.
