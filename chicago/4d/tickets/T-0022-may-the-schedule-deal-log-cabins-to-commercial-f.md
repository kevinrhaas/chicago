## Answered 2026-08-27 — YES, and the ticket's own premise is refuted

**The number.** Before this run, **15 invented buildings stood on South Water Street's line
and not one of them was log** — 13 of the 15 the party-line river row itself — against a
documented record for the same line of 8 buildings, one of them (`hogan_store`) log. All five
log dwellings the schedule had dealt the five South Water blocks were on the Lake face, put
there by the recipes' own face rule (*"the two meanest take Lake"*), which is a preference in
no source — and Lake is the town's OTHER principal thoroughfare, so the rule moved cabins from
one commercial frontage to another.

**The evidence, three witnesses.** (i) The committed record already stands log TRADE buildings
on the principal-street line: `hogan_store` (store, South Water), `philo_carpenter_log_shop`
(drug shop, Lake), `madore_beaubien_house` (dwelling and store, South Water), `mansion_house`
(tavern, Lake); one street back, `james_kinzie_house` is a documented log RESIDENCE on Lake.
(ii) Image 11 of the owner's brief of 2026-08-18 — *"South Water Street in 1834"*, the plate
T-0078 already cites for the party-line TREATMENT — draws the row as *"roughly ten one-storey
log and frame buildings shoulder to shoulder facing the river"*. (iii) The owner's ruling of
2026-08-27 on PR #371's fork, option (b).

**What shipped.** The rule is the recipe's new `placement_rule.frontage_fabric`, and it was
APPLIED: ten records changed places — five log dwellings into the South Water run, five frame
cottages out to the Lake lots the cabins held. No roof added or removed, no id, family,
footprint or form value changed, no household re-homed, no bake. `tools/measure_frontage_fabric.py`
holds it (red at the parent commit, green here) and `check.sh` runs it. Liberty **L182**.
