More docks: the river gets its working waterfront.

**The owner, 2026-08-18, verbatim: "you can add more docks!"** — said with the general
ruling that the loop has been *"too cautious on adding items to the scene. you are totally
fine to be liberal with adding reconstructed items when i ask for things, you can just
label and mark them as such."* This overrides the reading T-0041 shipped under (only the
docks stated in the ordinance clause, two of them). The override is the rule.

The reference is the 2026-08-18 brief (`data/sources/assets/owner_brief_2026_08_18/README.md`,
images 3 and 11): masts crowd the reach below the Dearborn drawbridge, and the South Water
frontage is a continuous working bank. A town shipping at that volume has more than two
landings.

Build reconstructed timber landings and wharf decks along the working banks — South Water
between the forks and the drawbridge first — at the reconstructed tier, each with a
LIBERTIES entry stating what bounded it (bank length, the 1834 view, the wharfing-out
practice T-0041 researched). Reuse the wharf path T-0041 built; T-0059 (a river-wharf mode
of pier_crib) and T-0058 (walkable decks) are the generator and walkable halves — build on
them, do not duplicate them.

**Acceptance:** standing on the south bank between Dearborn and the forks, the river reads
as a working frontage — several docks and landings, not two — and each new one cards as
reconstructed with its bounds. Gates green.
