**The source, 2026-09-05.** The owner added a second copy of J. S. Wright's manuscript survey of
1834 at `chicago/pre_fire_v1/maps/images/1834-wright-map.jpg` — the Historic Urban Plans (Ithaca) reproduction of the
**National Archives original**, 5050 × 6628 px at 600 dpi, with its own caption: *"Two portions are
missing, the larger being near the lower center … the manuscript was mounted on cloth to repair this
tear."* It is a different scan of the same drawing this project already traces as `wright_1834`
(the BPL/Leventhal copy, 4204 × 5166 px, IIIF `commonwealth:js957744g`). The owner's instruction:
*"review this and create tickets to update and enhance the map … several items from this map should
be incorporated including the streets, there are streets documented here that are missing, including
the blocks, where the public square is, where the various sloughs are, things like 'the kinzie block'
… look at all of the streets and block numbers … the lakeshore edge and that whole area … the whole
path of the river going south … note the sections as labeled in the legend."*

## The owner, 2026-09-05

*"where the various sloughs are."*

## What the project has

Three Main Branch sloughs and one north-side watercourse, all already read off Wright:

| id (terrain spec) | what | source |
|---|---|---|
| `lasalle_slough_lower` / `_upper` | the watercourse Wright draws dropping south off the main stem just east of La Salle | `wright_1834`, Conley/Stelzer; carried to the river on the owner's ruling (T-0129) |
| `state_slough_course` / `_mouth` | the South Division's drain, public square → Tremont site → river at State | chicagology, Wright (mouth), Conley/Stelzer |
| `north_side_slough` | *"existence and course documented by Wright 1834; width measured off the drafted band; depth invented"* | `wright_1834` |
| `west_prairie_swale_a` / `_b` | zone 18's *"1–2 ft slough swales"* | no source — placed |

Each of the Wright readings was made inside a tracing **window** of the BPL scan (the forks box,
the harbour box). The new sheet is whole.

## The ask

1. **Walk the entire registered sheet for drawn watercourses** — every line Wright draws that is
   water and not river: the two sloughs off the main stem, the north-side one, and anything in the
   West Division, in Wabansia, in the School Section or along the lake shore that the windows never
   covered. Count them. A count of four that matches what the town has is a result; a fifth is a
   finding.
2. **Re-check the three traced ones on the 600 dpi scan.** Where the new scan's drafted band
   disagrees with the traced course by more than the georeference's ±20 m, say which scan is
   believed and why. The La Salle slough in particular was *carried through to the river* on a
   ruling rather than a reading; if the new sheet shows the bulge the old one did, say so and let
   the ruling stand on the record.
3. **The two placed swales** (`west_prairie_swale_a/b`) either find a drawn line on the sheet and
   take it as their source, or stay `reconstructed` with the sheet's silence recorded — *"Wright
   draws no watercourse on the West Division's prairie"* is itself a fact worth one line.
4. Nothing here changes a course without a reading; this is the audit that decides whether T-0793
   and T-0794's traces need a slough ticket after them.

**Done when** every watercourse on the sheet is counted against the four the town has, the three
traced ones are confirmed or corrected on the new scan with the reason, and the two placed swales
carry either a source or the sheet's silence.
