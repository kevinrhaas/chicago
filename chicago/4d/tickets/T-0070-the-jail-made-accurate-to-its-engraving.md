County jail engraving in the brief (`data/sources/assets/owner_brief_2026_08_18/README.md`,
image 1).

near-flat or very low-pitched roof, minimal openings — a strongbox of a building — with
board fences lining the street beside it and a plank walk at the fence line. (The
street-edge treatment is T-0069's; this ticket owns the building.)

furniture; the jail record's current massing and construction may be generic). Bring its

than a generic cabin, and its card carries the plate as the reasoning for construction
and massing. Gates green.
