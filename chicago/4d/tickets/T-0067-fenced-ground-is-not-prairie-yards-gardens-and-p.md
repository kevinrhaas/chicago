Fenced ground is not prairie: yards, gardens and pens.

**The owner, 2026-08-18, verbatim: "everplace that is fenced in would have a different
ground, the wagon yard would probably be dirty dusty ground and fences around properties
inside the fence would not be wild prairie but curated lawn and garden or animal pens."**

The enclosure layer exists (T-0050 the Western Hotel wagon yard, T-0051 the estray pen,
T-0052 the dooryard pickets — all done) but the ground inside every fence still renders
as the same wild prairie as outside. Inside a fence, the ground should say what the fence
is for:

- **working yards** (the wagon yard, the estray pen): bare, dusty, hoof- and wheel-worn
  dirt, sparse trampled grass at the edges;
- **dooryards and gardens** (the picketed lots): curated ground — short green, garden
  beds/rows, path to the door (the Kinzie plate's fenced garden plots and brief image 12's
  fenced gardens are the references);
- **animal pens**: bare trodden earth.

Suppress the prairie flora scatter inside enclosure polygons and lay a per-type ground
treatment instead. Reconstructed tier, one LIBERTIES entry for the scheme.

**Acceptance:** enclosure interiors read differently from the prairie outside them — the
wagon yard is dust, a picketed dooryard is garden — visibly, from the walker, for each
enclosure type. Gates green.
