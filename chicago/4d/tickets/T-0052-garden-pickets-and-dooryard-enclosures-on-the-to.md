Garden pickets and dooryard enclosures on the town's house lots.

Piece 3 of 3 of **T-0038 — Fences and enclosures: the estray pen, wagon yards, garden pickets**, split because the parent needed more than one run's demonstration to be done. The parent keeps the full ask and its links; this ticket owns one slice of it.

**Acceptance:** the fenced garden plots the Kinzie-view plate shows are drawn on the town's house
lots as picket enclosures on the enclosure layer, from a record that states which lots and why,
with the treatment graded and claimed in `docs/LIBERTIES.md`.

**The evidence is a TREATMENT and not a location**, which is the whole difficulty and the reason
this is a ticket rather than a line in another one: `docs/ROADMAP.md` K5 (a) cites the Kinzie-view
plate for *"picket-fenced garden plots and Lombardy poplars"* as a reference for treatment, while
the house itself stays excluded. So the run that takes this has to say, in the record, on what
basis a particular lot gets a fence — and `data/exclusions.json` is to be read before adding
anything.
