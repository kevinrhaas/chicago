The seventh SCHOOLS column of 33S7-9YYJ-6H, No. of Scholars at public charge, is in the binding gutter and is recorded unread rather than blank.


The SCHOOLS, &c. block of `33S7-9YYJ-6H` prints seven columns. T-0629 bounded and swept six of
them. The seventh — **No. of Scholars at public charge** — is bounded on the left at `x3602` and
has **no right-hand rule at any coverage threshold down to 0.30**: the leaf curls into the binding
at about `x3640` and the gutter takes the rule, the cell and whatever is in it.

It is recorded as **unread**, not as blank, which matters because on this form a blank cell is a
zero and recording it blank would mint a nought nobody read. The band as swept carries 33 ink
components, essentially all of them binding shadow, and none is readable.
