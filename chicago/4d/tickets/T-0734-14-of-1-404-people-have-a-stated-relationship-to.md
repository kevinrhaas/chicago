14 of 1,404 people have a stated relationship to anybody else: the kinship the sources already print.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — the definition of done, never weakened to pass)

Found by the final resident audit (T-0512). Of 1,404 people, **14** carry a stated kin
relationship — wife, husband, son, daughter, child, brother, sister, mother, father — and
only 44 are recorded inside a household of more than one person at all. Everybody else is a
household of one, related to nobody.

Some of that is true: a letter list prints a name and nothing else, and this project will
not invent a family. But some of it is not. The corpus prints kinship it has already read —
Hurlbut on the Kinzie half-brothers (T-0597), the marriage entries in the St Cyr register,
the death notices Fergus prints with a surviving widow named, the 1840 census households
the bridge has already matched. Those relationships are documented and the household
records never took them.

**Acceptance:** the kinship the committed sources already state, written onto the
households that carry those people, each with its source and its grade; a count of how many
were available and how many landed; and the ones the sources do NOT support left alone and
said so. No relationship inferred from a shared surname.
