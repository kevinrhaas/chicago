Printed 232's continuation leaf is not in this deposit: find it in FamilySearch collection 1786457 or on the National Archives microfilm, and read the 31 households' industry, pension and schools cells.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — one demonstration, never weakened to pass)

T-0543 searched every right sheet in this deposit — 37 of them, 33 household continuations —
and none foots printed 232's free population of 193 (by its marks) or 195 (by its own footings)
on 31 entries. `continuation_search_page_232.json` records the search image by image and
`pages/33S7-9YYJ-W6.json` now carries `pairing.state: "unpaired_search_exhausted"`. The 31
households of printed 232 therefore have no family TOTAL, no industry columns, no pensioner
cell and no schools or illiteracy cells anywhere in what this project holds, and they are
recorded as ABSENT rather than unread.

This ticket is the only route left, and it is a SOURCE ticket rather than a reading ticket:

- The leaf is found — or shown not to be findable — outside these 74 images. Two routes are
  named and neither has been tried: FamilySearch collection 1786457, the collection these image
  ids belong to, recorded as login-walled and inaccessible (not absent) on 2026-09-03; and the
  National Archives microfilm of the 1840 population schedules, of which this project holds no
  roll and for which no roll number is asserted here, because nobody has looked one up.
- If a leaf is obtained it lands as a NEW SOURCE RECORD with its own provenance, is paired to
  printed 232 on both of T-0642's keys before a cell is read, and only then are the 31 lines
  read and closed against the sheet's own printed footings.
- If neither route yields it, that is the answer and it is recorded as one, route by route,
  with what was tried. The page file's `unpaired_search_exhausted` then stands as final rather
  than as pending.

Do NOT weaken this by filling printed 232's missing cells from its own left-sheet figures. The
left sheet gives ages and counts of persons; it does not give occupation, pensions or schooling,
and a total copied across is an invention wearing a measurement's clothes.
