Use the project's flora system and period ecology sources; distinguish species/community inference from exact-tree claims. The traditionally remembered cottonwood near the later 18th/Prairie area may be included only with its provenance and uncertainty made explicit.

Acceptance: the 1812 corridor visibly reads as lakeshore prairie rather than city blocks; vegetation respects water and terrain; exact landmark trees are separately sourced from reconstructed cover; and no 1880s street-tree or urban-yard vegetation leaks into the scene.
