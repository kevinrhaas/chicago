Norris's 1844 directory spent on the residents layer: every 1835 person the volume meets, shown on their card with the printed line, the page and the rule.

Piece 1 of 2 of **T-0569 — Norris's 1844 directory spent on the layers: the 1835 residents validated and enriched, and the businesses written and dated**, split because the parent needed more than one run's demonstration to be done. The parent keeps the full ask and its links; this ticket owns one slice of it.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — one demonstration, never weakened to pass)
- Every 1835 person Norris's 1844 directory meets on the crosswalk's rule — the single-entry
  matches, the ambiguous ones and the contested ones alike — is shown on their own card, with
  the line as printed, the printed page, the entry id and the rule that reached them.
- Nothing is regraded, moved, dated or given a trade or a street in 1835 by an 1844 line. The
  1844 reading is a layer BESIDE the household records, not a key inside them.
- What the 1844 line supplies that the 1835 record lacks is stated as a carry, and the split of
  a printed line into a trade and an address is not published as though it were a reading.
- The layer re-derives from the crosswalk under `--check`, and that check runs in `tools/check.sh`.
- Counts in the PR: people shown, matched, ambiguous, contested, refused, and what is carried.

**Not in this piece.** The businesses half of T-0569 is T-0588, and it waits on T-0567 (the
Historical Sketch and the Statistical Account) and T-0568 (the Advertising Directory): the
foundings that would date an 1844 firm at or before 1835 are printed in those two sections and
neither has been read yet.
