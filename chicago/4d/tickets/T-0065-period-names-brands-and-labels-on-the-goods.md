Period names, brands and labels on the goods.

**The owner, 2026-08-18, verbatim: "you can add period correct names and brands and
labels to things"** — the barrels, cases, sacks and crates the town already stacks
(T-0040's goods layer, the store fronts, the dock freight).

Give the goods painted and stencilled marks the way 1830s freight actually carried them:
the cooper's or mill's stencil on a flour barrel, "SALT", a merchant's name and port on a
shipping case, tare weights, a brand burned into a stave. Period-correct means: real 1830s
goods categories and plausible period merchant names (the town's own attested merchants
first — the store records already name them), period letterforms (stencil and signwriter
faces, not modern type), no anachronistic brands, nothing trademarked-modern.

Reconstructed tier throughout, one LIBERTIES entry for the labelling scheme stating its
bounds. Readable at walking distance; cheap on the budget (texture variants, not geometry).

**Acceptance:** goods at the stores and docks carry period-correct painted or stencilled
names and marks, legible when walked up to, with no modern or anachronistic branding;
carded reconstructed. Gates green.
