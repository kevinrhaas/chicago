The register of the Second Presbyterian Church of Chicago, 1842-92 (Grant), is cited on Newberry index cards for Chicago families and is not in this project's sources: find it, and read it back to the people who were here in 1835

**Found by T-0570** (the Newberry genealogical index, volume 1) and confirmed in its
hand-adjudicated sample: several Chicago cards cite `Chicago, Ill. Second Presb. Ch.,
1842-92. (Grant)` — the register of the Second Presbyterian Church of Chicago.

The project holds a church-register domain (`data/research/church/`) built for St Mary's
baptismal register and empty of everything else. A Presbyterian register beginning in
1842 is LATER EVIDENCE for an 1835 scene and is never an 1835 fact on its own — but it
names families, and the Second Presbyterian congregation was gathered out of the First,
which was organised in 1833 by people who were here.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — one demonstration, never weakened to pass)
- The work located, or recorded as inaccessible with the search written down.
- If reachable: a source record with `describes_date` stating the 1842-92 span, and the
  entries naming surnames this project holds read into `data/research/church/` in the
  T-0492 records shape.
- Nothing dated after 1835-07-01 reaches a resident record as an 1835 fact.
