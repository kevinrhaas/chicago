The churches of 1 July 1835 as establishments: the congregations the sources attest, each with its minister at the tier the layer holds and its sexton reconstructed, seated on the worship roofs the town already carries, and the census's fifth church left uninvented.

Piece 1 of 2 of **T-1411 — The churches, the schools and the press as establishments with their people: the five churches of the census with ministers attested and sextons reconstructed, the seven schools' teachers and pupil counts, the Democrat and the American staffed to the model, and the register's third printing office adjudicated**, split because the parent needed more than one run's demonstration to be done. The parent keeps the full ask and its links; this ticket owns one slice of it.

**Acceptance:** (stated before working — one demonstration, never weakened to pass)

1. Every congregation the sources reached attest as standing at Chicago on 1 July 1835 is
   a business record of class `church`, authored under `data/businesses/authored/`, seated
   on the committed roof its evidence allows.
2. Each carries its clergyman at the grade the resident layer and the sources hold for the
   OFFICE, with the day-level limit written on the row rather than folded into the grade.
   Where no source reached names a man, the record names none.
3. The census's five churches are not forced. A fifth congregation is invented for no count,
   and the shortfall is stated with the date caution the crosswalk is read with.
4. The crosswalk's `church` row names the records instead of standing empty, and T-0988's
   ruling that the row is not compared is left alone.
5. `./tools/check.sh` green and the smoke parts `smoke_budget.mjs --for-diff` names green.

**What the title asked for and the evidence refused, recorded rather than quietly dropped:**
the title says "its sexton reconstructed", written before the trade tables were read. The
only sexton any table this project holds ever prints is ONE — `sexton, St. James' Church`,
Fergus 1839, four years and some three thousand people after the scene, in a town with more
congregations than this one. Reconstructing four would invent four town offices against one
printed. So acceptance 2 stands as written, no sexton is drawn, the schema is deliberately
given no word for one, and each record's `replaceable_by` names what would create the office.
Documented zeros stand — the parent ticket asks for that in the same breath as it asks for
the staff.
