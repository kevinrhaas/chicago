The sward grows up through the Green Tree's plank walk.

Seen while shipping **T-0082**: the frontage layer lays a plank walk along the Green Tree's two
street walls, and the flora layer knows nothing about it, so grass and forbs stand up through the
deck. The walk reads as a path mown through a meadow rather than as boards laid on the ground.

It is general rather than local — the same will happen under every walk T-0069 lays, and it is the
first time anything in this project has put a hard surface on ground the sward already covers. The
enclosure layer does not have the problem because a fence is a line, not a surface.

**Acceptance:** no plant is drawn standing through a frontage deck, decided from the record rather
than by nudging a scatter seed — the flora layer takes the walks' footprints as ground it does not
plant, the way it already takes water and roadway — and the `south_water` critic frame is unchanged
outside the Green Tree's block. State what the exclusion costs in plant count.
