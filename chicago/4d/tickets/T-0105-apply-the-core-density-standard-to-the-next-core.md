Apply the core density standard to the next core block below the bar.

The succession T-0079 owes, on the T-0028 pattern: T-0079 raised the standard (three party-line
units per platted lot, measured at the smallest lot on the committed grid; a run may be anchored
on either end of its face so a corner lot builds to the corner) and demonstrated it on ONE named
block, `blk_lake_clark`. The core is not finished, and the run that closes this one files the next
if any core block still stands below the bar.

**Which block is next, and it is a fact about the schedule rather than a preference.** With the
standard in force, the platted blocks that are `open` and carry free lots on their business face
are `blk_south_water_lasalle` (3 free lots, 8 roofs of headroom), `blk_randolph_dearborn` (3 free,
8), `blk_south_water_franklin`, `blk_south_water_clark`, `blk_south_water_dearborn`,
`blk_lake_franklin` and `blk_randolph_market` (2 free, 4 each). Read
`data/reconstruction/1835_665_roof_programme.json` at the time of the run rather than this list:
the schedule is derived from what stands and moves under every parcel.

**Acceptance:** one named core block is dealt and built to the density standard — its business
frontage tightened toward party-line spacing, its corner lot built to the corner where it has a
free one, its improved lots carrying their outbuildings — with a before/after pair from the same
stand, the 665-roof total unmoved, both gates green, and the successor ticket filed if any core
block remains below the bar.
