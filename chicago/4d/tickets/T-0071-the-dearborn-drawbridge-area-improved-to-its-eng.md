The Dearborn drawbridge area, improved to its engravings.

**The owner, 2026-08-18, verbatim: "the whole bridge area needs to be improved. also
note the boats there."**

Two engravings in the brief show the 1834 Dearborn Street drawbridge
(`data/sources/assets/owner_brief_2026_08_18/README.md`, images 2 and 3): the
**gallows-frame form** — twin timber A-frames standing above the deck, chains down to
the lifting leaves — timber approach spans, low warehouses on the banks, masts crowding
the reach, a light/beacon structure toward the mouth. If the current model is a plain
flat span, it is the wrong bridge.

Scope: the bridge's own form (gallows frames, chains, leaves, railings), the approach
banks and their build-up (T-0046, bridge approach earthworks, is open and adjacent —
coordinate or take it first if it is still on the queue), and the immediate surroundings
that the engravings attest (bank structures at the reconstructed tier). The boats in the
reach are T-0063's — leave the water to it. The deck stays walkable end to end (T-0045's
machinery, done; T-0001's whole point).

**Acceptance:** from either bank approach, the Dearborn bridge reads as the engravings'
gallows-frame drawbridge — frames, chains, leaves — and remains walkable end to end;
new bank furniture cards at its honest tier. Gates green.
