**Owner, 2026-09-17:** the research is done; this band is the CHECK AND PREP before the city
gets built. "Make them good but not great."

This folds **five** tickets — T-1161 (population), T-1162 (occupations), T-1163 (households
and families), T-1164 (lodging and institutions), T-1165 (arrival and origin). Each file keeps
its evidence, marked `withdrawn` and naming this ticket; its brief becomes a SECTION here.

**Why one ticket.** All five answer one question — *what did the town of 1 July 1835 look
like, beyond the people we can name?* — from one body of evidence, with one method: take the
known population, compare it to what a lake port of roughly 3,200 people implies, and state
the difference. Five tickets means five runs each re-reading the same ledgers, re-deriving
the same known counts and re-arguing the same comparanda before writing a paragraph. The
comparison is the work and it is done once.

**This is the input to the order book (T-1166), and the order book is what bands 3-5 spend.**
The model does not need to be right to the person. It needs to be defensible, bounded, and
finished.

**Sections, each a page, not a paper:**

1. **Population** — how many people, by sex, age band and division. Known, modelled, and the
   gap between them.
2. **Occupations** — how many of each trade, profession, office and employment a port this
   size supports, against how many are attested.
3. **Households and families** — household types and sizes, and the rule that gives a head a
   wife and children when no source names them.
4. **Lodging and institutions** — the hotels, taverns and boarding houses, the fort and the
   vessels, and how many people they hold.
5. **Arrival and origin** — when the town's people came, from where, and why, as a
   distribution rather than per person.

**Acceptance, and the tolerance is the point:**

* Every figure is **a range with a stated method and a source for its comparanda**. A range is
  the ANSWER, not a hedge — a single number nobody can defend is worse than a bounded one.
* Every section says what it is NOT claiming, in one sentence.
* Anything the evidence cannot bound is written as an open question in the section that owns
  it. **It does not become a ticket.**
* **One run.** If a section cannot be finished in it, ship the other four and say which one is
  thin and why. Four good sections and a named gap beat five perfect ones that never land.

**Stop condition:** the five sections exist, the order book can be built from them, and the
model is closed. It will be revised when reconstruction finds it wrong, which is the honest
way round — not polished before anything has been built on it.
