About 130 named articles of the Chicago Indian trade, itemised in the American Fur Company's own book: rule on what the town may show and letter nothing without it.

**What is in hand.** Hurlbut closes his American Fur Company chapter with "a catalogue of
goods furnished for trade of the Chicago country, fifty-three years ago" — about 130 named
articles out of the company's own book: cloth, blankets, capotes, roram hats; northwest
guns, flints, powder, ball, beaver and duck shot; covered copper kettles, half axes,
cartouche and scalping knives, needles, thread, awls; beads, brooches, gorgets, arm bands,
black and white wampum, ear bobs, looking glasses, vermillion; and flour, pork, salt,
pepper, barrel biscuit, snuff, tobacco, highwines and whiskey. It is filed verbatim as
`bk_afc_018`.

**Why it needs a ruling before it needs a model.** This is the first itemised answer this
project has to what an Indian-trade store here actually held, and the temptation is to
letter a signboard or fill a shelf out of it. Two things forbid doing that by feel. It is
about 1828, seven years and one treaty before the scene. And Hurlbut says it is for "the
trade of the Chicago country" — a district, not a shop on South Water Street. Read beside
`bk_afc_005` (Hubbard bought the company's entire Illinois interest in 1828) and
`bk_afc_012` (Hubbard: "this place never had been preeminent as a trading-post"), the
honest answer may well be that the town shows LESS of this than it does now.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — one demonstration, never weakened to pass)
- A written ruling, in `docs/LIBERTIES.md`, on what the 1835 scene may show of the Indian
  trade and on what evidence — including the possibility that the answer is "less".
- Every existing signboard, shelf, pack or trade-goods asset in the town audited against
  it, with each either sourced or listed as a liberty. An unsourced one that survives the
  audit survives named, not silently.
- Nothing lettered, modelled or added from the 1828 catalogue without the ruling above it.
- If geometry moves, `tools/bake.sh --only` in the same commit and `check.sh` green.
