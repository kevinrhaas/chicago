## Interpretation

These totals measure named or otherwise source-identifiable records, not all historical structures.
Aggregate stock estimates are stored separately because surviving sources often provide a count without names or parcels.
The 17,450 structures reported lost in the Great Chicago Fire must not be treated as 17,450 individually identified records.
Unknown fire fate is preserved as unknown; no blanket 1871 termination is applied outside a sourced fire-footprint or direct account.
