# Research gaps and next passes

## Highest priority

1. Georeference the 1839 harbor plan, 1853 Hart map, and a high-resolution 1871 burnt-district map against documented control points.
2. Reconcile historical addresses through street-name and house-number change tables before assigning parcel geometry.
3. Resolve possible duplicate identities in the Du Sable–Le Mai–Kinzie residence chain and early Beaubien/Dean/Lee houses.
4. Expand contemporary city directories, tax lists, fire-insurance material, newspapers, church archives, and public-works contracts into row-level assertions.
5. Create a separately versioned property/parcel table; buildings that moved or were raised should retain one entity with effective-dated site and elevation events.

## Fire analysis

Digitize the fire footprint and attach a method field to every spatially inferred loss. Direct-source losses should remain distinguishable from polygon-derived losses. The three commonly reported section totals—West, South, and North divisions—are aggregate checks, not substitutes for building identities.

## Media

The current package includes public-domain or free-to-use historical map derivatives with source URLs and credits. Building photographs and engravings should be added only after item-level rights review, with creator, date depicted, date created, repository identifier, and a link back to the source record.
