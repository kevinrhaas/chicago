# Pre-1830 research notes

This tranche separates an evidenced building identity from a period-wide stock count. It therefore includes named buildings, complexes, and a few explicitly enumerated compound components, but does not invent identities for the unnamed remainder in retrospective counts.

Key unresolved entity problems are marked `needs_review=true`: the Du Sable/Le Mai/Kinzie house versus the 1826 Kinzie-Wolcott double log house; the Beaubien cabin versus the former Dean or Lee house; and the tenth building in the ten-building Du Sable compound. Forts are represented as complexes until component-level evidence can support barracks, blockhouses, magazines, and service buildings separately.

The Marquette record is an occupation/shelter episode. It should never be rendered as a precise cadastral footprint. Approximate coordinates are supplied only where a present-day landmark site is well established.
