## Findings that change the 1904 model
- **Robbins 2126**: photographed complete for Inland Architect May 1905 (bare trees) → shell finished by
  winter 1904–05; the Hamill (Tucker-built, late-1860s Van Osdel) house on the lot was razed in 1904.
- **2140 Tucker/Smith**: three roof states — cresting-crowned towers (pre-1881 stereo; 1898 book; a
  post-1890 photo beside the Murdoch house) and tall conical spires (another post-1890 photo). Date the
  spire photo before fixing the 1904 roofline.
- **2108/2110 coach house**: a single pre-1888 Kimball barn spanned the rear of both lots (party wall
  1888); in 1904 it stood whole. Reid's coach house dates from 1910 (not 1904).
- **Wheeler–Kohn**: copper SE bay and stone porch (1880s) present in 1904; heavy bracketed cornice was
  still there in 1904 (removed later); Kohn family in residence to 1908.
- **Prairie Ave roadway, 2100 block, 1891**: 32 ft curb to curb (Tribune via Glessner blog).
- **2027**: Tribune 1898 — "a wide, rambling, red brick house" (Cobb with the Walkers).
