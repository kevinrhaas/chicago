# Request: George Glessner's photographs of Prairie Avenue (c. 1888–1901) — Glessner House Museum

**To:** info@glessnerhouse.org (the address on the museum's contact page)
**Subject:** Permission request — George Glessner photographs for the Chicago Building Atlas
(Prairie Avenue, 1904)

---

Dear Glessner House Museum,

I am building the Chicago Building Atlas (https://chicago.polecat.live/), a free, non-commercial,
source-cited research reconstruction of Prairie Avenue as it stood in 1904. Your blog *The Story of
a House* has been one of its most valuable sources, and every item we draw from it is credited to
the museum and linked to the post.

George Glessner's photographs from about 1888 to 1901 are the most direct evidence we have of the
street in that period. We currently only link to them. With the museum's permission, we would like
to show them in the atlas's image viewer at web resolution (about 1400 px), each credited to the
museum and linked back to your post. If you can share higher-resolution scans, we would be grateful
for those too.

Two further questions, if you have a moment. We would be glad of the answers either way:
- Is the winter view of the Dexter house at 1721 also George's? A copy circulates on other sites.
- Are there other Glessner family photographs of the street, from about 1885 to 1911, that the
  museum would be willing to see used this way?

The credit line we would use is: "Glessner House, Chicago — photograph by George Glessner." If you
prefer different wording, or have terms or a fee, please let me know.

With thanks for the museum's work and for the blog,
Kevin Haas
Chicago Building Atlas — https://chicago.polecat.live/
