**To:** archives@artic.edu (Ryerson & Burnham Art and Architecture Archives). The address is the one

Dear Ryerson & Burnham Archives staff,

I am building the Chicago Building Atlas (https://chicago.polecat.live/), a free, non-commercial,
source-cited research reconstruction of Prairie Avenue as it stood in 1904. It credits every image
to its holder and links back to the catalogue record.

Second Presbyterian Church (1936 S. Michigan Avenue; James Renwick, 1874; rebuilt after the 1900
fire) is one of the district's anchor buildings. I would be grateful for your help with two things.

**1. Permission to reproduce eight frames of the Renwick drawings on Burnham Library microfilm
roll 26.** Your catalogue marks these "contact the Archives". The drawings date from 1874, and
Renwick died in 1895. We would show them at web resolution (1400 px), each with full credit and a
link to your record:
