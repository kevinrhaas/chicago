Present-day measured-photo brief

Photograph current fabric as a separately dated measurement layer. Obtain the museum’s restoration chronology for replaced doors, glazing, roof/copper, rainwater goods, paving, vines and service stairs before carrying any feature into 1904.

  1. Whole elevations: straight-on Prairie/east, 18th/north, west/stable and accessible south boundary, plus both corners of each. Keep the sensor level; record camera position, compass direction, height and focal length. Use moderate focal lengths where space permits; retain the wider frame needed to locate every close-up.
  2. Courtyard: east- and west-looking full views, then overlapping images around its perimeter and into each corner. Aim for 60–80% overlap, fixed exposure/focus within each sequence, and common control points on both walls and ground. Include bow, turret, curved steps, gate and stable in connected sequences.
  3. Roof: museum-arranged elevated viewpoints covering ridge/valley junctions, stack backs/caps, dormers, cupola, gutters and downpipe heads. Pair every detail with a wider locating view. Photograph any occluded junction explicitly as occluded; do not infer it from a filled-in panorama.
  4. Details and scale: door leaves open/closed where available, hinge/lock/grille profiles, inscription and capitals, window/reveal sections, light wells, rainwater attachments, paving transitions. Include a measured scale in the same plane; record a few checked overall dimensions and their endpoints. Photograph a gray/color reference under the same light for each material set.
  5. Light and season: diffuse-light material views plus controlled raking-light relief views. Record date, time, weather and wet/dry condition. Separate leaf-on/leaf-off coverage; present-day planting is not a substitute for a dated 1904 garden record.
  6. Deliverables: original camera files and full-resolution JPEGs, EXIF preserved, no perspective correction or AI reconstruction baked into the masters. Supply a camera-location sketch, measurement log, photo IDs (for example GH_20261009_E_001), and a feature register stating original/restored/replaced/unknown with the restoration source. Keep corrected derivatives separate and state the correction.

Method and limits

The bounded pass used the supplied catalog URLs, holder APIs/download links and catalog-linked IIIF services; retried failed public routes; searched the two Encyclopedia identities and the Barford/Nickel leads; and read the IIT and Yale catalogs. Retrieved photographs and relevant PDF pages were visually inspected. Textual newspaper records were read as transcriptions, not scans of the original newspaper pages. No restricted file, authentication challenge or paid service was bypassed.

The four Barford download payloads are literal InternetShortcut files pointing at about:blank. Collection dates are not individual exposure dates. The Nickel API’s tiny placeholder is not sufficient for a visual ruling. Federal custody does not establish public-domain rights for contributed photographs or sketches: #165 has been changed from public domain to unknown/link-only, preserving the former claim in correction history.
