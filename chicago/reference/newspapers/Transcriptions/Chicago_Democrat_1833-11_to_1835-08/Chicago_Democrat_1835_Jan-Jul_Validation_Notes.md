# Chicago Democrat, January-July 1835: Validation Notes

## Scope and release standard

The source is a 39-page, image-only PDF. It contains ten publication units: eight complete numbered weekly issues, one four-page Extra, and one numbered issue represented by only three scanned pages. The deliverables are high-accuracy, OCR-assisted research transcriptions, not fully diplomatic editions. Bracketed passages should be checked against the scan before scholarly quotation.

## Method

- Rendered pages at high resolution without altering the archival PDF.
- Inspected mastheads and continuation cues to establish issue boundaries.
- Segmented pages according to their visible column rules and irregular layouts.
- Compared independent Apple Vision and Tesseract recognition passes.
- Reconciled text against enlarged page images, with separate attention to personal names, place names, dates, monetary figures, legal sections, and repeated standing matter.
- Retained historical spelling and visibly printed metadata.
- Used `[uncertain: ...]`, `[illegible]`, and `[missing/obscured]` rather than guessing where the scan does not support a secure reading.
- Performed an independent cross-issue review before packaging.

## Residual limitations

Dense nineteenth-century type, weak impressions, clipped or bound edges, bleed-through, display advertisements, and physical damage prevent complete certainty in some passages. The square-bracket system makes those areas searchable and distinguishes tentative readings from unrecoverable loss.

## Per-issue review findings

- **January 21 and March 25 Extra:** independent review corrected masthead metadata, resolution ordinals and vote totals, appointments, legal-section readings, and the Extra's page-8 association. Residual risk is concentrated in clipped columns, dense Post Office lists, and photographed pointer/hand losses. The canal act was checked section-by-section against the enlarged newspaper and an enacted-text parallel; the newspaper controlled any variant.
- **May 20:** an independent audit rejected the first seven-way crop set because it split real columns and caused reordered or missing blocks. A final original-resolution check established six physical columns. The delivered file was rebuilt from 24 corrected crops with true single-column Apple Vision and Tesseract passes. Because the parallel reviewers reached their run limits during this last rebuild, every column is conservatively marked uncertain pending another line-level human pass.
- **May 27, June 4, and June 10:** all 72 physical columns received corrected single-column Vision and independent Tesseract readings. The most difficult curved outer columns were widened and manually reconciled. A planned final independent review did not complete after reviewer run limits were reached; dense advertisements, name lists, land descriptions, and numerical tables remain the main risks.
- **June 17:** an independent reviewer mapped the photographed-hand obstruction, recovered readable text above it, verified the internal `CHICAGO, JUNE 10, 1835` line, and supplied exact Canal Tolls figures plus extensive name/place corrections that were applied. Page 25's genuinely covered portions remain explicit gaps.
- **June 24, July 1, and July 8:** each supplied column received independent Vision/Tesseract recognition and enlarged spot checks. Final second-review passes did not complete after reviewer run limits were reached. July 8 ends with an explicit missing-page notice and must remain designated partial.
