TERMS.
I. The Chicago Democrat is printed on an imperial sheet, and afforded to subscribers at TWO DOLLARS AND FIFTY CENTS, payable in ADVANCE.
II. Any person procuring six subscribers or over, and forwarding the amount of the subscriptions for the same, will be entitled to a copy gratis.
III. ADVERTISEMENTS.—For 17 lines or under, $1 for the first, and 25 cents for every subsequent insertion. A liberal discount will be made to those who advertise by the year.
