## Method and evidence discipline

The source sweep used repository sources plus exact/variant-name web and local-history searches. Letter-list evidence is treated as postal/resident-candidate attestation under the project ruling, not as proof of household, occupation, kinship, ethnicity, migration origin, or a specific 1 July 1835 roof. Same-name matches were not promoted without date/place discriminators. Competing spellings and possible duplicates remain candidates. Negative searches are retained as documented no-corroboration outcomes and are not evidence of nonexistence.
