# T-0493 — the four Chicago voter lists of 1833-1835

**Researched:** 2026-09-03. **Cohort:** not people but ENTRIES — the 345 printed rows
of four lists, each read as it stands and then crosswalked to the residents layer.

## What was read

`chicago/reference/voter-roll/Early_Chicago_Voter_Lists_1833-1835_Transcription.txt`
(with its .docx and the supplied PDF beside it): the Genealogy Trails republication of
Ingrid Latimer Schulz's IRAD transcription, published in *Chicago Genealogist* XXV/4
(Summer 1993). Four lists:

| list | what it is | entries |
|---|---|---|
| `poll_1833` | poll list, first election of the Board of Trustees, 10 August 1833 | 30 |
| `tax_1833` | tax list of the Town of Chicago, 1833 | 115 |
| `poll_1834` | election returns, poll list of 1834, filed 11 August 1834 | 115 |
| `poll_1835` | the 1835 poll list — it prints no date | 85 |

**345 entries, 214 distinct surnames.** The ticket said 346 and 215. The deposit prints
345, the supplied PDF prints 345, and the live Genealogy Trails page prints 345: the
1833 poll list is thirty names, not thirty-one. The measured figure is the one carried
everywhere in this package.

## The outcome, entry by entry

|  | entries | matched | candidate | unmatched |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| poll_1833 | 30 | 17 | 2 | 11 |
| tax_1833 | 115 | 31 | 6 | 78 |
| poll_1834 | 115 | 36 | 6 | 73 |
| **poll_1835** | **85** | **15** | **68** | **2** |
| all four | 345 | 99 | 82 | 164 |

`matched` means one resident of that surname agrees with the entry's forenames initial
for initial and no other does. `candidate` means either two residents agree equally, or
none does and the v1 workbook's bridge rows carry the entry verbatim. `unmatched` means
neither. **Of the 85 men on the 1835 poll, 70 reach no resident** — and those 70 are the
sweep below.

Surname-level, for comparison with the ticket's own measure: 108 of the 214 surnames
match a resident surname, and 41 of the 1835 poll's surnames do. A surname is a clue,
not an identity, which is why the entry-level figures above are the ones that count.

## The second-source sweep

All 70 searched in Andreas vol. 1, whose full OCR text was fetched from archive.org
(sha256 recorded in `data/research/civic/search_log.json`) and searched for the name as
the book sets a name — a forename or initial standing immediately in front of the
surname, case-sensitive. **36 hit, 19 have the surname but never behind a forename the
poll prints, 15 are clean negatives.** Every search is in the log, positive and negative
alike; each hit carries its verbatim passage.

Three further sources were probed and none produced evidence:
the Illinois State Archives public-domain land tract sales database was **inaccessible**
(HTTP 403 to one request, a read timeout to another — the database is up and will not
answer this runner, and that is not an absence); **earlychicago.com did not resolve**;
Genealogy Trails Cook County is reachable but is the publisher of the very transcription
being read and is therefore **not independent**.

## What this package does NOT do

It mints nobody and regrades nobody. Under the owner's grading ladder a man on the 1835
poll with a second independent source is `attested` and a man on the poll alone is
`inferred` — applying that is T-0514 and T-0515, from T-0513's consolidation. A hit in
Andreas says the book names a man of that name; whether he is the man on the poll is an
identity judgement nobody has made yet.

## Unresolved, and left open on purpose

- **The 1835 poll list's date.** Andreas contradicts himself: "The third election was
  held in July, 1835" in the town chapter, and "the third election of town oHicers,
  which occurred August 5, 1835" in the police chapter. The scene date is 1 July 1835,
  so the list is either within days of it or thirty-five days after. Not decided here.
- **Twenty-eight or thirty.** Andreas says twenty-eight men voted at the first election;
  the poll list prints thirty names. Not reconciled.
- **The tax list is not a property roll.** The ticket hoped to read it as a ranked one.
  As published it carries names only — no valuation, no lot, no figure of any kind — so
  it ranks nobody. The figures, if they survive, are on the IRAD original, which nobody
  in this project has seen.
