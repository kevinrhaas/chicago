## Method
The pass used stable person IDs and conservative exact-name/variant searches. A source had to provide useful chronological and geographic evidence before it was retained. Same-name records with competing geography were preserved as candidates rather than merged. No household, address, roof, kinship, occupation, ethnicity, or immigration fact was inferred from surname alone.
