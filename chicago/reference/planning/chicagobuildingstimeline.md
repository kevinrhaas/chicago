# Chicago Buildings: A Year-by-Year Timeline

**2,135 named buildings and structures, 1803 – 2030,** ordered by year of completion.

549 of them are gone. Where the year a building came down is documented it follows the name after a dagger — `†1931` — with the cause when it was something other than an ordinary demolition. A bare `†` means the building is known to be gone but the year has not been pinned down.

**On coverage.** The early years are the point of this list, so they are as close to complete as the surviving record allows: through the 1860s it names essentially every structure documented in the historical record, down to individual taverns, cabins, and frame churches. From the 1870s forward — once Chicago was putting up thousands of buildings a year — it shifts to named landmarks, skyscrapers, churches, schools, theaters, parks, industrial works, housing developments, and civic buildings. Both world's fairs are included in full, since each was an entire city that stood for a season and then vanished.

Two companion files go with this one: a CSV carrying the same data as structured fields (address, designation, demolition cause, alternate names) and a source registry listing the archives, datasets, and APIs to pull from to build this out into a complete database.
