# Historic Image Reference: Chicago and Peer Cities, 1800-1849

Compiled 2026-08-17. Drawings, engravings, lithographs, watercolors, paintings and the era's first daguerreotypes showing **building exteriors, street scenes, riverfront, docks/harbor and town panoramas** with scenes depicted between 1800 and 1849. Chicago is the primary subject; five peer cities of the same era are collected for comparative reference, one directory per city.

A scene *depicted* pre-1850 qualifies even when the artwork is later (an 1884 Andreas engraving of an 1833 building is in scope); both dates are recorded on every entry. Plat/survey maps, portraits and interiors are excluded.

**About `licensed_previews/`:** these are publicly served preview or thumbnail images (some watermarked) from commercial licensing archives — Getty Images, Granger, Alamy, Bridgeman — and from auction records (Invaluable, LiveAuctioneers, Hindman, etc.). They are stored at preview quality for research reference. The *underlying artworks* are public domain (all pre-1930), but the agencies license their high-resolution scans; item IDs and any visible pricing are recorded so full-resolution versions can be licensed if needed. Auction photos additionally document original artworks in private hands that no library holds.

Every entry lists: title, creator, medium, the date of the **scene** depicted, the date the artwork was **made**, holding institution, rights basis, and the source page it came from. "Also held/licensed by" lines point to commercial archives selling scans of the same artwork.
